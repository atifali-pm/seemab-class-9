Assalam o alaikum Seemab. Aaj hum density seekhenge. Lekin pehle ek sawal, aur is ka jawab aap zindagi bhar nahin bhoolengi.

Socho ek chhoti si loha ki keel, jo aap ke hath mein aa jaye. Aap us ko paani ke tub mein daalein. Kya hoga? Woh seedha neeche chali jayegi. Doob gayi.

Ab socho Karachi ki bandargah par khara ek poora jahaz. Loha hi loha. Hazaaron ton. Us mein gaariyan bhi hain, log bhi hain, saaman bhi. Woh doobta kyun nahin?

Ajeeb baat hai na. Chhoti si keel doob gayi, aur us se laakhon guna bhaari jahaz paani par tairta reh gaya. Is poore sabaq ka jawab isi sawal mein chhupa hai. Thora sa sabr karein, hum wapas is jahaz par aayenge.

Chalein pehle ek aur cheez dekhte hain. Ammi se kahein ke ek kilo rui la dein, aur ek kilo loha. Dono ka wazan barabar hai, ek ek kilo. Lekin rui ka itna bara thaila banega ke aap ki god mein mushkil se aayega, aur loha? Ek chhota sa tukra, mutthi mein aa jaye ga.

Dono ka wazan barabar, magar jagah alag alag. Kyun? Kyunke loha ke andar chhote chhote zarray, atoms, bilkul paas paas, ghichi ghichi bhare hue hain. Jaise school assembly mein sab larkiyan bilkul juri khari hon. Aur rui mein? Wohi zarray door door hain, beech mein hawa hi hawa. Jaise chhutti ke baad ground, jahan sab bhaag rahi hain aur beech mein bohat jagah hai.

Bas yehi density hai. Density ka matlab hai: ek jagah mein maal kitna thonsa hua hai. Bhaari hona aur dense hona do alag baatein hain.

Ab kitab ki definition, jo imtihan mein likhni hai, ghor se suniye: Mass per unit volume of the substance is called its density. Aur formula hai rho equals m over V. Rho density, m mass, V volume.

Dekha? Formula bhi wohi keh raha hai jo hum ne rui aur lohe mein dekha: kitna maal, kitni jagah mein.

Ab ghar mein isi ki teen misalein aap ko roz nazar aati hain.

Pehli. Paratha banate waqt tawe par tel daalein aur saath paani ka chhinta lag jaye. Tel hamesha upar aa jata hai. Kyun? Kyunke tel ki density zero point nau hai aur paani ki ek. Tel halka hai, upar aa gaya.

Doosri. Garmiyon mein glass mein barf daalein. Barf hamesha upar tairti hai, neeche nahin baithti. Kitab ki table kehti hai barf nau sau bees, paani ek hazaar. Barf paani se kam dense hai. Isi liye sardi mein jheelon ki upar wali tah jamti hai aur neeche machhliyan zinda reh jati hain. Agar barf bhaari hoti, to poori jheel neeche se jamti aur saari machhliyan mar jatin. Ek chhoti si density ki wajah se poori zindagi bachi hui hai.

Teesri. Sona. Aap ki ammi ki sone ki chooriyan. Sona unnees hazaar teen sau. Yani paani se unnees guna zyada dense. Isi liye chhoti si chuuri bhi hath mein bhaari lagti hai. Aur isi liye jaali sona pakarna asaan hota hai: agar wazan halka lage, to sona nahin hai.

Ab wapas us jahaz par chalein, jo hum ne shuru mein chhora tha. Jawab ab aap khud de sakti hain. Keel thos loha hai, andar kuch nahin. Us ki density lohe wali, saat hazaar nau sau. Paani se bohat zyada. Doob gayi. Lekin jahaz? Jahaz andar se khaali hai, us mein hawa bhari hui hai. Loha aur hawa dono ko mila kar, poore jahaz ki density paani se kam ho jati hai. Is liye woh tairta hai. Jis din us mein paani bhar jaye, density barh jati hai, aur wohi jahaz doob jata hai. Titanic ke saath yehi hua tha.

Ye baat zindagi bhar yaad rakhein: doobna ya tairna wazan par nahin, density par hai.

Ab do minute ka hisaab, kyunke imtihan mein number isi ka milta hai.

Liquid ki density nikalni ho to: khaali cylinder ka mass naapein, liquid daal kar dobara naapein, farq nikalein, yehi liquid ka mass hai. Volume cylinder par parh lein. Phir mass taqseem volume. Misal: khaali cylinder ek sau bees gram, liquid ke saath ek sau saath. To liquid chalees gram. Volume pachaas millilitre. Chalees taqseem pachaas barabar zero point aath gram per millilitre.

Aur seedhi shakal wale solids ka volume: cuboid ka length into width into height, cube ka side ka cube, aur sphere ka chaar bata teen pi r cube.

Ek aakhri baat jo aksar aadha mark khati hai: units milayein. Mass kilogram mein ho to volume cubic metre mein, jawab kilogram per cubic metre. Mass gram mein ho to volume cubic centimetre mein. Kilogram ko cubic centimetre se kabhi divide na karein.

To aaj ki kahani ka khulasa: keel doobi, jahaz taira, rui bari magar halki, sona chhota magar bhaari, tel upar, barf upar. Sab ka ek hi jawab: density. Kitna maal, kitni jagah mein.

Khuda hafiz, aur agli dafa hum dekhenge ke pathar jaisi tedhi cheez ki density kaise naapte hain, us mein ek pyaasay kawway ki kahani bhi hai.
