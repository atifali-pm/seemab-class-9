Assalam o alaikum Seemab. Pichli dafa maine wada kiya tha ke aaj pyaase kawway ki kahani se shuru karenge. To suniye.

Garmi ka din hai. Ek kawwa udte udte thak gaya, pyaas se bura haal. Us ko ek matka mila, jis ki tah mein thora sa paani tha. Chonch andar daali, paani tak nahin pohanchi. Aage aap ko yaad hai. Us ne aas paas se chhote pathar uthaye aur ek ek kar ke matke mein daalne shuru kiye. Paani upar aata gaya, aur kawway ne pee liya.

Ab ek sawal, jawab abhi mat dijiye. Paani upar aaya kyun? Matke mein to ek boond paani nahin barha. Phir level kis ne uthaya?

Aur ek sawal, is se bhi bara. Eent ka volume nikalna asaan hai, length into width into height. Lekin pathar? Us ki koi seedhi side hi nahin. Us par footi rakh kar naapengi kya? Ye dono sawal yahin lataka hua chhor dein.

Chalein ghar chalte hain. Ek glass mein paani bharein aur dekhein ke level kahan hai. Ab us mein ek chamcha daal dein. Paani upar aa gaya, bilkul utna jitni jagah chamche ne ghairi. Chamcha aa gaya, paani ko hatna para.

Bas yehi trick hai. Hum pathar ko naapte hi nahin. Hum paani se poochte hain ke tum kitna hate, aur paani khud bata deta hai.

Ab kitab ke alfaaz, section saat nuqta do nuqta do, page ek sau bahattar par: "We can find the density of irregular shaped solid objects like stones which can sink in the water. We need graduated measuring cylinder to measure volume and balance to measure mass of object."

Chhe steps, isi tarteeb mein. Pehla: find the mass of the irregular shaped stone, balance se. Doosra: add some water in measuring cylinder and measure its initial volume. Teesra: tie thread with the object and lower it in the measuring cylinder. Chautha: water will rise, yani displace, and measure the final volume. Ye paani aur pathar dono ki reading hai. Paanchwan: volume of object barabar final volume minus initial volume. Chhata: density nikalne ke liye mass ko volume se divide karo. Equation saat nuqta chaar: Density of Solid equals Mass of Solid over Volume of Solid.

Ab do sharten, aur inhi par aadha number jata hai. Kitab ka aakhri jumla: any insoluble irregular shaped object. Pehli shart, cheez paani mein doobe. Doosri, woh insoluble ho, yani paani mein ghule nahin.

Kyun? Cheeni ki dali ghul jayegi, level uthega hi nahin. Thermocol upar tairta rahega, level poora nahin uthega. Lekin ammi ka chamcha, ghar ki chabi, ek pathar: ye doobte bhi hain aur ghulte bhi nahin. Ye sahi hain.

Ab wapas kawway par. Us ne koi jadoo nahin kiya tha. Har pathar ne matke mein apni jagah maangi aur paani ko upar dhakel diya. Woh kawwa displacement method kar raha tha, bina kitab parhe.

Aur pathar ka volume? Side naapne ki zaroorat hi nahin. Level parh lein, pathar daal dein, dobara parh lein, farq nikal lein.

Ye baat zindagi bhar yaad rakhein: jo cheez paani mein doob jati hai, woh paani ko bilkul utna hi upar uthati hai jitni woh khud bari hai. Paani jhoot nahin bolta.

Ab do minute ka hisaab. Example saat nuqta chaar, page ek sau teyhattar. Pathar ka mass assi nuqta bawan gram. Paani athara millilitre se chhiyalis millilitre par aa gaya. Volume barabar chhiyalis minus athara, yani athaais millilitre, aur ek millilitre barabar ek cubic centimetre, to athaais cubic centimetre. Density barabar assi nuqta bawan taqseem athaais, barabar do nuqta athasi gram per cubic centimetre.

Yahan dhyan se suniye. Kitab ne khud athaais nikala, magar neeche assi nuqta bawan taqseem pachees likh kar jawab teen nuqta bais chhap diya. Ye kitab ki ghalti hai. Aap hamesha athaais se divide karengi, jawab do nuqta athasi.

Ab aaj ka doosra hissa, aur ye aap dekh chuki hain. School assembly: sab larkiyan qatar mein, bilkul juri hui, bas zara sa hil rahi hain. Yehi solid hai. Classroom break se pehle: paas hi hain magar ghoom phir rahi hain, ek doosre ke paas se guzar rahi hain. Yehi liquid hai. Aur chhutti ki ghanti: sab ground mein bhaag rahi hain, beech mein bohat jagah, kabhi takra bhi jati hain. Yehi gas hai.

Kitab ki definition: "Matter exists in three fundamental states; solid, liquid and gas. Gaps between these particles is the main reason that divides the matter in three states. Particles of matter apply an attractive force on each other. The difference in strength of this force is the reason for smaller or large gaps between particles."

Table saat nuqta do ka asaan tareeqa: sirf ek cheez badal rahi hai, attractive force. Solid mein strong, liquid mein intermediate, gas mein negligibly weak. Baqi sab usi se nikal aata hai. Solid: closely packed, fixed lattice pattern, sirf mean position par vibrate, fix shape, high density, low energy. Liquid: randomly arranged magar paas paas, flow and glide over each other, no fix shape, medium density, greater energy. Gas: faasla particle ke size se bhi bara, tez random motion, no fix shape aur no fix volume, low density, highest energy.

Ek aakhri baat, aap ke apne chhape hue safhe ki. Figure saat nuqta chaar mein labels ulat gaye hain. Jis cylinder par Solids likha hai us mein particles door door phaile hain, aur jis par Gases likha hai us mein ghichi hue paas paas hain. Usool yaad rakhein: jahan particles sab se paas paas hain wohi solid hai. Tasveer dekh kar nahin, usool dekh kar jawab likhein.

Khuda hafiz, aur agli dafa matter ki chauthi halat, jis ka naam plasma hai. Woh sooraj ke andar hai, aasman ki bijli mein hai, aur bazaar ki neon sign mein bhi. Nazar aane wali kaainat ka ninanwe percent plasma hai.
