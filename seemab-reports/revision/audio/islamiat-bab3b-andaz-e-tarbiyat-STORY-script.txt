Assalam o alaikum Seemab. Aaj ka manzar bhi Masjid-e-Nabawi ka hai, lekin is dafa jo hone wala hai woh tumhein jhatka de ga.

Ek dehati, sehra ka rehne wala aadmi, masjid mein aata hai aur ek konay mein peshab kar deta hai. Masjid-e-Nabawi mein, jahan namaz hoti hai.

Sahaba karam razi Allahu anhum ka ghussa socho. Aaj hamari kisi masjid mein aisa ho to kya hoga, tum jaanti ho.

Ab dekho Nabi kareem sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne kya kiya. Logon ko rok diya. Phir paani ka dol talab farmaya, aur masjid pak kar di gayi. Na shor, na daant.

Us aadmi ne baad mein khud bataya: Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam mere paas aaye, mujhe na daanta, na bura bhala kaha, bas itna farmaya ke ye masjid aisi jagah hai ke is mein peshab nahin kiya jata, ye to Allah ta'ala ke zikr aur namaz ke liye taameer ki gayi hai. Sunan Ibn Majah, hadees paanch sau untees.

Woh aadmi wahin deen samajh gaya aur zindagi bhar ye waqiya sunata raha. Agar us din daant pari hoti, to woh bhaag jata aur kabhi wapas na aata. Yehi andaz-e-tarbiyat hai.

Kitab ka buniyadi usool ye hai ke Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne khud amal kar ke dikhaya, phir logon ne us kaam ko apni zindagi ka hissa banaya. Numayan ausaf do: hikmat aur baseerat. Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam mukhatib ki istidad saamne rakhte, be-mahal guftagu na farmate, aur mauqa mahal ka khususi lihaz farmate. Teen lafz pakke karo: khud amal, hikmat, baseerat.

Ab saat tareeqe, har ek ke saath uska waqiya.

Pehla. Naam liye baghair naseehat. Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam kisi ki ghalti par sar-e-aam uska naam le kar ya baat tok kar tarbiyat na farmate, balke umoomi andaz mein naseehat farma dete.

Doosra. Narmi aur mohabbat. Yehi dehati wala waqiya, Sunan Ibn Majah, hadees paanch sau untees. Agar foran mutanabba karna hota, to intehai narmi se samjhate, taake mukhatib haq baat qabool karne par aamada ho jaye.

Teesra. Shafqat, aur aib joi se parhez. Hazrat Anas bin Malik razi Allahu anhu farmate hain: main das baras tak Huzoor sallallahu alaihi wasallam ki khidmat mein raha, jo kaam main ne jis tarah bhi kar diya, Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne kabhi ye nahin farmaya ke ye kyun kiya, aur jo na kar saka us par ye nahin farmaya ke ye kyun nahin kiya. Sahih Muslim, hadees do hazaar teen sau nau. Das saal, ek bachha, aur ek dafa bhi daant nahin.

Chautha. Ikhtisar, yani jawami ul kalim. Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam taweel waaz se gurez farmate aur bari baaton ko mukhtasir jumlon mein bayan kar dete. Allah ta'ala ne Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ko jawami ul kalim, yani jame kalimat ki sifat ata farmai thi.

Paanchwan. Mizaj aur nafsiyat ka lihaz. Ek shakhs ne arz kiya, qayamat kab aaye gi. Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne dekha ke us par qayamat ki fikr tari hai, to jawab dene ke bajaye khud sawal farmaya: tu ne us din ke liye kya tayyari kar rakhi hai. Sahih Bukhari, hadees chhe hazaar ek sau sarsath. Is khush usloobi ne sail ko ehtisab-e-nafs par aamada kar diya.

Chhata. Hosla afzai. Ek shakhs mulaqat ko aaya. Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne uski hatheliyon par nishanat dekhe aur wajah poochi. Us ne kaha, main mazdoor hoon, sakht mehnat se ye nishanat par gaye hain. Ye sun kar Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne shafqat farmate hue uske haath choom liye. Ek mazdoor ke khurdure haath, aur Nabi kareem sallallahu alaihi wasallam unhein choom rahe hain. Aise iqdamat se dil mein mohabbat paida hoti hai, aur insan har haal mein us baat par gamzan rehta hai.

Saatwan. Tadrij, yani qadam ba qadam. Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ek khami door farmate aur ek khoobi parwan charhate. Hazrat Muadh bin Jabal razi Allahu anhu ko Yemen bhejte waqt farmaya: ae Muadh, tum ahl-e-kitab ke paas ja rahe ho, pehle unhein tauheed aur risalat ki dawat dena. Jab maan lein to batana ke Allah ne din raat mein paanch namazein farz ki hain. Phir batana ke zakat farz hai, jo unke maaldaron se le kar unhi ke fuqara mein taqseem hogi. Sahih Bukhari, hadees ek hazaar teen sau pachanway. Sab kuch ek hi din mein nahin, ek ke baad ek.

Ab isay apni zindagi par laagu karo. Koi dost ghalti kare to poori class ke saamne uska naam mat lo, alag le ja kar kaho. Arsh kuch tor de, to pehla jumla ye kyun kiya na ho. Aur koi tumhein narmi se samjhaye, to samajh lena ke woh Sunnat par amal kar raha hai.

Ab imtihan ki baat. Is sabaq ki mashq kitab ke page athattar par hai, aur sawal do ke teen sawal wohi hain jo tumhare quiz mein hain.

Pehla. Mizaj aur nafsiyat ka lihaz, misal de kar. Qayamat wala poora waqiya, jawabi sawal usi tarah, hawala Sahih Bukhari, aur natija ke sail ehtisab-e-nafs par aamada ho gaya.

Doosra. Islah o tarbiyat ke liye kis tarah guftagu farmate the. Char cheezein: be-mahal guftagu nahin, naam liye baghair naseehat, narmi aur mohabbat, aur ikhtisar yani jawami ul kalim.

Teesra. Sahaba karam razi Allahu anhum ki hosla afzai kaise farmate the, misal de kar. Mazdoor wala poora waqiya, haath choomna, aur akhri jumla ke is se dil mein mohabbat paida hoti hai.

Aur zindagi bhar ke liye ek baat. Baat theek karni hai, insan ko toorna nahin. Sach keh dena asaan hai. Is tarah kehna ke saamne wala qabool bhi kar le, yehi tarbiyat hai.

Khuda hafiz Seemab. Isi ke saath Bab teen khatam. Agli dafa Bab chaar shuru hoga, akhlaq o aadab, aur pehla sabaq hai shukr o qanaat: kam par razi rehne wala asal mein sab se ameer kyun hota hai.
