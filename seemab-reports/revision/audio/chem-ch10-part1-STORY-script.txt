Assalam o alaikum Seemab. Aaj Chemistry Chapter 10, acids aur bases. Ye chapter aap parh chuki hain, aaj hum ise kahani ki tarah pakka karenge. Pehle ek sawal, jo aap ki apni rasoi mein khara hai.

Socho Ammi ne mez par do glass rakhe hain, dono mein saaf paani jaisa liquid, dekhne mein zara sa bhi farq nahin. Ek mein nimbu nichora gaya hai, doosre mein sabun ghola gaya hai. Chakhna mana, chhuna bhi mana. Aap ke paas sirf litmus paper ki ek patti hai.

Pehle glass mein neeli patti daalein, wo foran laal ho jati hai. Doosre mein laal patti daalein, wo neeli ho jati hai.

Ab sawal. Dono glasson mein zyada tar to paani hi hai. Phir andar aisa kya ghula hua hai, jo aankh ko nazar nahin aata magar kaghaz ka rang badal deta hai? Zara sabr karein, hum in glasson par wapas aayenge.

Pehle rasoi ki sair. Nimbu khatta, imli khatti, aur sirke ki bottle kholein to boo se naak chadh jaye. Ye sab acids hain, aur acid ki pehli pehchan yehi hai: sour taste. Ab doosri taraf: sabun, shampoo, toothpaste. Sabun wala paani haath par phisalta hai, jaise koi cheez mutthi se chhooti ja rahi ho, aur zabaan par lagay to kadwa. Ye bases hain: bitter aur slippery.

Dono corrosive hain, isi liye chakhna aur chhuna mana. Aur dono ke solutions bijli guzarte hain. Table 10.1 mein bas yehi paanch lines hain: taste, blue litmus, red litmus, skin, aur electrical conductivity. Trick: B se Base, B se Blue. Base laal kaghaz ko neela karta hai, acid ulta.

Ab asal sawal: nimbu, imli aur sirka teen alag cheezein hain, un mein common kya hai? Sweden ke Svante Arrhenius ne 1887 mein yehi poochha, aur jawab paani ke andar mila.

Kitab ki definition, English mein, jo imtihan mein hubahu likhni hai: An acid is a substance that ionizes in water to produce H plus ions, and a base is a substance that ionizes in water to produce O H minus ions.

Ionize ka matlab hai tootna, biscuit ki tarah: poora biscuit pehle, tukre baad mein. Isi liye poora molecule hamesha arrow ke baain taraf hota hai aur toote hue ions daain taraf. Jaise H N O three, arrow, H plus jama N O three minus.

Aur yahan wo cheez hai jo pichle paper mein do marks le gayi thi: equation ulti likh di gayi thi, H plus jama N O three minus, arrow, H N O three. Tukre pehle aur biscuit baad mein. Aisa kabhi nahin. Usi paper mein sirke wale acid ke tukre bhi adhoore the. Us ka naam C H three C O O H hai aur sirf aakhri H alag hota hai, to bacha hua hissa poora likhein, C H three C O O minus, charge ke saath. Aur H two S O four se do H plus nikalte hain, kyunke us mein H do hain.

Ab do aur glass, dono mein acid. Sirka aap salad par daal leti hain, magar hydrochloric acid ka qatra haath par gire to jal jata hai. Farq ginti ka hai. H C l paani mein jaye to us ka har molecule toot jata hai, sau ka sau: strong acid. Sirke wala acid jaye to sau mein se sirf paanch toot-te hain: weak acid.

Kitab kehti hai: An acid that ionizes completely in aqueous solution is called a strong acid. Likhne mein farq sirf arrow ka hai. Strong ke liye ek seedha arrow, weak ke liye do tarfa arrow, jo batata hai ke kuch toot rahe hain aur kuch wapas jur rahe hain. Strong: H C l, H N O three, H two S O four, aur bases mein N a O H, K O H, calcium hydroxide. Weak: sirke wala acid aur ammonia.

Ek aakhri lafz, alkali. Kitab: A base which is soluble in water is called an alkali. Yani wo base jo paani mein ghul jaye, jaise sabun. Copper hydroxide nahin ghulta, to wo base to hai magar alkali nahin. Yaad rakhein: har Lahori Pakistani hai, magar har Pakistani Lahori nahin.

Ye sab roz aap ke saamne hain. N a O H se sabun banta hai, yaad rakhein Na se nahane ka sabun. K O H se shaving cream. Calcium hydroxide, yani chuna, mistri ke plaster aur mortar mein. Magnesium hydroxide wo safed dawa hai jo pet ki jalan par Ammi deti hain, kyunke pet ka acid H C l hai aur ye base us ko thanda kar deta hai. Acids mein H C l metal saaf karta hai, H N O three khaad mein, aur H two S O four lagbhag har cheez mein.

Ab wapas un do glasson par, aur jawab aap khud de sakti hain. Pehle glass mein nimbu ke acid ne paani mein H plus ions chhor diye hain, doosre mein sabun ke base ne O H minus. Ye ions itne chhote hain ke aankh ko kuch nazar nahin aata, magar litmus paper inhein pehchan leta hai.

Ye baat zindagi bhar ke liye rakh lein: paani mein H plus tairay to acid, aur O H minus tairay to base. Baqi sab tafseel hai.

Do minute imtihan ke. Ionization ki equation mein teen cheezein marks deti hain: molecule baain aur ions daain, state symbols bracket mein g, l, s ya a q, aur sahi arrow. State symbols apne alag aadhe marks rakhte hain. Aur jo bhi number likhein, us ke saath unit zaroor: pichle paper mein ek jagah unit reh gayi thi aur aadha mark wahin gaya.

Khuda hafiz. Agli dafa chocolate ki kahani, aur naya sawal: baking soda par sirka daalein to jhaag ka toofan kyun aata hai, aur wo gas kahan se aati hai jo kisi ne daali hi nahin thi.
