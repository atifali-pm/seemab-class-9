Assalam o alaikum Seemab. Aaj Chapter 10 ka doosra hissa, aur pichli dafa wala sawal.

Ammi cake bana rahi hain. Pyaali mein maida, cheeni aur chutki bhar baking soda. Phir ek chammach sirka daalti hain, aur agle lamhe poora batter jhaag ban kar oopar chadh jata hai, jaise pyaali mein chhota sa jwalamukhi phoot para ho.

Ab sawal. Us jhaag mein gas hai, magar gas kisi ne daali nahin thi. Ye gas kahan se aayi? Hum is pyaali par wapas aayenge.

Socho aap ke paas chocolate hai aur saheli ke paas nahin, aur aap apni chocolate usay de deti hain. Do kirdar ban gaye: dene wali aur lene wali. Yehi poora idea hai.

Chemistry mein wo chocolate ek proton hai, yani H plus. 1923 mein Bronsted aur Lowry ne yehi baat kahi. Kitab ki definition, English mein, jo imtihan mein hubahu likhni hai: According to this theory, an acid is a proton donor and a base is a proton acceptor.

Dekhein: H C l jama H two O, arrow, H three O plus jama C l minus. H C l ne apna H de diya, to wo acid. Paani ne le liya, to paani base. Jo bane wo hain hydronium ion aur chloride ion.

Aur yahan wo trap hai jo pichle paper mein ek mark le gaya tha. Sawal tha ke is reaction ka acid kaun hai, aur jawab likha gaya H three O plus. Wo acid nahin: H three O plus to product hai, aur bana hi is liye ke kisi ne usay H diya. Acid hamesha wo hai jis ne diya, yani H C l, arrow ke baain taraf. Donor ko dekhein, product ko nahin.

Pehchanne ka aasan tareeqa: H gino. Baain wali cheez daain par ban kar kya hui, H kam hua ya barha? H C l bana C l minus, H kam hua, chocolate di: acid. H two O bana H three O plus, H barha, chocolate li: base.

Ab ek aur reaction, aur yahan hairani hai. Ammonia, N H three, wohi teekhi boo wali cheez jo cleaner mein hoti hai. Paani mein daalein: N H three jama H two O, do tarfa arrow, N H four plus jama O H minus. H gino: N H three bana N H four plus, H barha, to ammonia base hai. Paani bana O H minus, H kam hua, to yahan paani acid hai.

Pichle paper mein ek MCQ mein paani ko base likh diya gaya tha. Paani ka pakka role hai hi nahin: H C l ke saath wo chocolate leta hai, N H three ke saath deta hai, us saheli ki tarah jo saathi dekh kar badal jati hai. Kitab is ka naam deti hai: Substances that react with both acids and bases are called amphoteric substances. Aur ghor karein: ammonia mein ek bhi O H nahin, phir bhi wo base hai. O H minus aaya zaroor, magar paani se.

Ab acids karte kya hain? Teen kaam. Ek, metal ke saath: acid metal ko khaa jata hai aur hydrogen ke bulbule nikalte hain. Zinc jama do H C l, arrow, zinc chloride jama H two. Do, carbonate ke saath: salt, paani aur carbon dioxide, wohi jo Ammi ki pyaali mein hua tha. Teen, base ke saath: salt aur paani, jise neutralization kehte hain, aur pet ki jalan par antacid yehi karta hai.

Bases ka apna khaas kaam bhi hai: garam karne par wo ammonium salt ko tod dete hain aur ammonia gas nikalti hai. N a O H jama N H four C l, arrow, N a C l jama N H three jama H two O. Ammonium salt se ammonia, naam mein hi likha hai.

Aur bases aate kahan se hain? Metals ke oxides aur hydroxides se. Metal jalayein to oxide banta hai: char N a jama O two, arrow, do N a two O, yani sodium oxide. Phir oxide paani mein daalein to hydroxide banta hai: calcium oxide paani mein daalo to bartan garam ho jata hai aur calcium hydroxide banta hai, wohi chuna jo deewar par lagta hai.

Ab wapas Ammi ki pyaali par. Jawab aap khud de sakti hain. Sirke ke acid ne apna proton baking soda ke carbonate ko de diya. Carbonate itna H sambhal nahin sakta, to foran toot gaya, paani aur carbon dioxide mein. Carbon dioxide ko bahar bhaagna tha, aur bhaagte hue us ne batter ko jhaag bana diya. Gas kisi ne daali nahin thi, wo wahin bani, sirf ek proton ke safar se.

Ye baat zindagi bhar ke liye: har acid base reaction mein sirf ek chhoti si cheez safar karti hai, ek proton. Jo deta hai wo acid, jo leta hai wo base, aur dono arrow ke baain taraf khare hote hain.

Do minute imtihan ke. Equation ke baad har atom ginein. Aap ki kitab khud teen jagah balance karna bhool gayi hai, misal ke taur par N a two C O three jama H C l. Sahi ye hai: N a two C O three jama do H C l, arrow, do N a C l jama H two O jama C O two. Do jagah two lagta hai, aur pichle paper mein yehi two reh gaya tha, poora mark chala gaya. Gin lein, tees second lagte hain. Aur oxygen hamesha jori mein, O two, akeli O kabhi nahin.

Doosri aadat: gas aur product ka naam English mein bhi likhein. Sirf C O two nahin, carbon dioxide. Sirf N H three nahin, ammonia. Sirf M g O nahin, magnesium oxide. Har naam aadha mark hai.
