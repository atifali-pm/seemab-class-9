Assalam o alaikum Seemab. Aaj ka sabaq kisi definition se shuru nahin hoga. Aaj hum seedha ek manzar mein chalte hain, Madina Munawwara mein.

Socho. Masjid-e-Nabawi ke saath ek chhota sa hujra. Andar Nabi kareem sallallahu alaihi wasallam tashreef farma hain. Wohi hasti jinke ek lafz par poora Arab uth kar khara ho jata tha.

Darwaze par aahat hoti hai. Hazrat Fatima az-Zahra razi Allahu anha andar tashreef laati hain. Unki apni beti.

Hona kya chahiye tha. Beti aayi, baap baitha rahe, muskura de, baat khatam. Hamare gharon mein aksar yehi hota hai.

Lekin jo hua, woh Hazrat Aisha razi Allahu anha khud bayan farmati hain. Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam khade ho jate. Khush aamdeed kehte. Unka haath pakarte. Unki peshani ka bosa lete. Aur apni jagah par unhein bithate. Hawala kitab ne khud diya hai: Shuab ul Iman, hadees aath hazaar nau sau sattaees.

Ruk kar ye manzar dobara dekho. Duniya ke sab se baray insan apni beti ke liye khade ho rahe hain. Char kaam, aur charon nazar aane wale. Yehi is sabaq ki jaan hai: izzat dil mein rakhne ki cheez nahin, kar ke dikhane ki cheez hai.

Ye ittefaq nahin tha, ye ek hukm par amal tha. Allah ta'ala ne Quran-e-Majeed mein irshad farmaya: wa aashiroohunna bil maroof. Tarjuma, aur unke saath husn-e-sulook ka bartao karo. Ye Surah An-Nisa ki ayat unnees hai. Isay zabani yaad kar lo, kyunke har jawab ka aaghaz isi se hoga.

Ab char farman, har ek ke saath uska hawala.

Pehla. Tum mein se behtareen log woh hain jo apne ghar walon ke saath achha bartao karte hain, aur main tum mein se apne ghar walon ke saath behtareen bartao karne wala hoon. Jami Tirmizi, hadees teen hazaar aath sau pachanway.

Doosra. Jis ne do betiyon ki parwarish ki yahan tak ke woh jawan ho gayin. Phir Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne apni do ungliyan mila kar farmaya: main aur woh qayamat ke din is tarah qareeb honge. Sahih Muslim, hadees chhe hazaar chhe sau pachanway. Sirf betiyon ko achhi tarah paalne par Jannat mein Nabi kareem sallallahu alaihi wasallam ka saath.

Teesra. Jiski ek beti ho, aur woh use zinda dafan bhi na kare, uski tazleel bhi na kare, apne bete ko us par fauqiyat bhi na de, to Allah ta'ala uske badle use Jannat mein dakhil farmaye ga. Sunan Abi Dawood, hadees paanch hazaar ek sau chhialis.

Chautha, aur sab se pyara. Maa ki khidmat ko lazim pakro, kyunke Jannat uske qadmon mein hai. Sunan Nasai, hadees teen hazaar ek sau chhe.

Ab do aur manzar, dono chhote, dono bhulaye na jaane wale.

Hazrat Halima Sadia razi Allahu anha, Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ki razaee walida. Jab woh milne tashreef laayin to Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne unke ehtram mein apni chadar bichhai, aur bohat der tak unse guftagu farmate rahe. Unhon ne bachpan mein doodh pilaya tha. Barson baad, poore Arab ke Nabi ne unke liye apni chadar zameen par bichha di.

Aur teesra. Khawateen ne arz kiya ke mard Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam se zyada seekhne ki wajah se hum se aage nikal gaye hain, hamare liye bhi koi din muqarrar farma dijiye. Aap sallallahu alaihi wasallam ne ek din ka wada farmaya, aur us din khawateen se mulaqat farmate aur unhein taleem dete. Larkiyon ki taleem ka poora ek din, Madina mein, chaudah sau saal pehle.

Ab is ko apni zindagi se joro, Seemab.

Kitab saaf likhti hai ke kya nahin karna. Khawateen par aawazein kasna, ghoor kar dekhna, unka mazaq urana, khawateen ke taleemi idaron ke bahar ghair zaroori tehelna, aur unke raaston mein baithna. In paanchon se ijtinab. Aur kya karna hai: awami maqamat par sharm o haya, tehzeeb aur aala akhlaq ka muzahira, aur mulazmat pesha khawateen ka zyada ehtram, kyunke woh apni zimmedariyon se barh kar kaam kar rahi hoti hain.

Tumhare school mein iska matlab kya hai. Jab koi kisi larki ki shakl par, uske kapron par ya uski baat par hansta hai, to ye sirf badtameezi nahin, ye us usool ke khilaf hai jiske liye Nabi kareem sallallahu alaihi wasallam khud khade ho gaye the. Aur ghar mein, jab bhai ko pehle mile aur behen ko baad mein, to teesri hadees yaad kar lena.

Ab do minute imtihan ki baat. Is sabaq ki mashq kitab ke page chauhattar aur pachhattar par hai, aur sawal do ke teen mukhtasir sawal wohi hain jo tumhare quiz mein hain.

Pehla. Fatima az-Zahra razi Allahu anha ki aamad par kya sulook, hawale ke saath. Charon kaam ginao: khade hona, haath pakarna, peshani ka bosa, apni jagah bithana. Phir hawala. Hawala chhoot gaya to aadha number gaya.

Doosra. Do betiyon wale ke baare mein kya irshad farmaya. Do ungliyan mila kar wala jumla zaroor likhna, warna baat adhoori hai, aur hawala Sahih Muslim.

Teesra. Khawateen ke ehtram ke liye kya iqdamat aur inki zaroorat kyun hai. Paanch ijtinab, phir awami maqamat wala jumla, aur akhir mein wajah: khawateen ke ehtram ke baghair koi muashra taraqqi ki manzilein tay nahin kar sakta. Ye akhri jumla likhe baghair jawab poora nahin hota.

Aur ek baat zindagi bhar ke liye. Izzat woh nahin jo dil mein rakhi jaye. Izzat woh hai jo khare ho kar dikhai jaye.

Khuda hafiz Seemab. Agli dafa ek dehati Masjid-e-Nabawi mein aisa kaam karega ke tamam sahaba ghusse mein uth khare honge, aur Nabi kareem sallallahu alaihi wasallam ka jawab tumhein hairan kar dega.
