# Seemab's study plan, Class 9

Written 24 September 2026. This is the working plan for finishing the whole Class 9 course. It is kept in the repo so any session, or any person, can pick it up without digging through memory.

## The idea in one paragraph

Every evening Seemab gets short notes for two subjects. Each note covers one topic (or a few related ones in the big subjects), runs to 2 pages, or 3 for Maths and Chemistry, and ends with a 3-question quiz whose answers are not printed. She attempts the quiz herself. When a subject's notes are finished she sits one exam for that subject, and that paper is built mostly from the quiz questions she has already practised. The aim is steady daily progress she can enjoy, not pressure.

## The daily shape

- **Two subjects a night, two notes each**, so four notes, Monday to Saturday.
- **Sunday is light**: one subject, two notes. Notes yes, exam never.
- One thinking subject (Maths, Chemistry, Physics) paired with one reading subject (Islamiat, Biology, Quran) wherever the counts allow.
- Notes for the next set go out only after she submits the current attempt.

## The notes format

- 2 pages maximum, 3 for Maths and Chemistry.
- One topic per note, even if it fills one page. A topic too big for one sitting is split.
- **English carries the content**: definitions in the book's exact words, technical terms, formulas, equations, table contents, worked examples, and the quiz questions. **Roman Urdu carries the explanation**: why it matters, how to remember it, what a step is doing, the everyday example, the warning about a book error. Islamiat and Quran keep their substance in Urdu script, since those papers are written in Urdu.
- No chapter map, no emoji legend, no dictionary, no score guide, no long introduction.
- Ends with a box headed simply **"Quiz"**: 3 questions, **no answers in the PDF**. The answer key lives in an HTML comment in the source, for marking and for building her paper.
- Book misprints that could cost her marks are flagged in a red box, with the wrong version struck through.

## Standing rules worth repeating

- Nothing is ever set outside her own textbook, and every question needs a stated learning outcome behind it.
- Every paper is independently re-solved by a second pass before it is sent.
- Every marked attempt gets a second, blind marking before the score is final.
- Results go out as a Roman Urdu message with each lost mark named by question number, plus the report and the key.
- Nothing is sent to her without being asked for, each time.
