🍃 Hissa 1: Gas Exchange in Plants

8.3 Gas Exchange in Plants (kitab p121)

Pehle ek asaan baat. Hum saans lete hain to ek hi jagah se, naak se. Paudhe ke paas naak nahin hoti. Paudhe ka har cell apni gas exchange khud karta hai, apni zaroorat ke mutabiq. Koi pump nahin, koi lungs nahin, bas gas khud ba khud zyada wali jagah se kam wali jagah ki taraf chali jati hai. Isi ko diffusion kehte hain.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz: exam mein yehi likho Gas exchange is the type of respiration that occurs at organismic level. Gas exchange is when the entire organism exchanges gases with the environment.

Kitab is ka maqsad bhi batati hai: "The goal is to make sure that the organism must be provided with oxygen which is needed for cellular respiration, where complex organic compounds are broken down, and carbon dioxide is produced which is to be removed in outer environment."

  • "Every cell in a plant does its own gas exchange, depending on what it needs."
  • Ye exchange khaas tor par do openings se hota hai: stomata (tiny openings in leaves, patton ke chhote chhote sooraakh) aur lenticels (small openings in stems, tanon ke chhote sooraakh).
  • "This exchange happens through a process called diffusion."
🧠 Do lafz yaad rakhne ka tareeqa Stomata mein "S" hai: Subz patta (leaf). Lenticels mein "L" hai: Lakri wala tana (stem). Bas.
✨ Ek line mein Gas exchange = poore organism ka mahol ke saath gas ka len den, organismic level ki respiration. Har cell apna kaam khud karta hai, stomata (leaves) aur lenticels (stems) ke zariye, diffusion se.

8.3.1 Pattern of gas exchange in leaves (Photosynthetic parts) (kitab p122)

Kitab: "The plants being photosynthetic autotrophs show different pattern of gas exchange in day and night through the leaves."

Matlab: patte din mein ek tarah se gas lete dete hain, aur raat mein bilkul ulta.

Table 8.1: Pattern of gas exchange between plant and environment (poori table, sirf 2 lines hain, zaroor yaad karo):

In daytimeIn nighttime
Inhale (andar lena)Carbon dioxide (CO2)Oxygen (O2)
Exhale (bahar chhorna)Oxygen (O2)Carbon dioxide (CO2)

Din mein kya hota hai. Kitab: "During the day, plants are busy with both photosynthesis (making food) and respiration. The rate of photosynthesis varies throughout the day as it mainly depends upon light intensity. Generally, the rate of photosynthesis is greater than rate of respiration, therefore, the photosynthesis needs more carbon dioxide than what respiration produces, so plants bring extra carbon dioxide from the environment. On the other hand, photosynthesis produces more oxygen than respiration needs, so plants release excess oxygen."

🏠 Ghar ki misal Socho ghar mein roti ban rahi hai aur saath saath khai bhi ja rahi hai. Din mein banane ki raftaar khane se zyada hai, is liye aata (CO2) bazaar se mangwana parta hai, aur bachi hui roti (O2) bahar bhej di jati hai. Raat mein banana band, sirf khana chalta hai.
⚠️ Sab se bara ghalat fehmi Bohat log kehte hain "din mein paudha sirf photosynthesis karta hai aur raat mein sirf respiration". Ghalat. Respiration chobees ghante chalti rehti hai. Din mein sirf photosynthesis ki raftaar zyada hoti hai, is liye net mein CO2 andar aur O2 bahar nazar aata hai.

Figure 8.10: Pattern of gas exchange in leaves and roots in day and night. Tasveer mein ek darakht hai. Upar ki taraf "In day" par CO2 andar aur O2 bahar ke teer hain, aur "At night" par O2 andar aur CO2 bahar. Zameen ke neeche jaron par dono waqt ek jaisa hai: O2 andar, CO2 bahar.

Raat mein kya hota hai. Kitab: "At night, plants act more like animals. They absorb oxygen and release carbon dioxide because there's no photosynthesis happening in the dark."

Compensation point of photosynthesis (kitab p122)

Ab subah saweray (dawn) aur shaam dhalte waqt (dusk) ki baat. Us waqt roshni halki hoti hai. Photosynthesis ki raftaar gir kar theek respiration ki raftaar ke barabar aa jati hai.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz: exam mein yehi likho During dawn and dusk, when light intensity is low, the rates of photosynthesis and respiration become equal. This means the carbon dioxide produced by respiration is enough for photosynthesis, and the oxygen released by photosynthesis is used in respiration. At this point, there's no net exchange of gases with the environment, and we call it the "compensation point of photosynthesis."
🧮 Samajhne ka tareeqa Jitni CO2 respiration banati hai, utni hi photosynthesis kha jati hai. Jitni O2 photosynthesis banati hai, utni hi respiration kha jati hai. Dono ka hisaab barabar, is liye mahol ke saath koi net len den nahin. Gas andar hi andar ghoomti rehti hai.
🎯 Pakki trick: "no net exchange" ka matlab Compensation point par na CO2 absorb hoti hai, na O2 absorb hoti hai (mahol se). Ye MCQ 6 ka seedha jawab hai: option D. Yaad rakho: "compensation" ka matlab hi hai barabar kar dena.

8.3.2 Pattern of gas exchange in roots (non-photosynthetic parts) (kitab p122)

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Roots also exchange gases with soil air through root epidermal cells which involves absorption of oxygen and release carbon dioxide because there's no photosynthesis happening in the roots and it remains same day and night.
  • Jarein mitti ki hawa (soil air) se gas exchange karti hain.
  • Ye kaam root epidermal cells karte hain.
  • Jarein sirf respiration karti hain, photosynthesis bilkul nahin (wahan roshni hi nahin).
  • Is liye jarein hamesha O2 leti hain aur CO2 chhorti hain, din ho ya raat, pattern badalta nahin.
🪴 Roz ki misal Gamle mein zaroorat se zyada paani daal do to paudha murjha jata hai. Wajah: mitti ke sooraakh paani se bhar gaye, jaron ko oxygen mili hi nahin. Jaron ko bhi saans chahiye.
✨ Ek line mein Patte: din mein CO2 in aur O2 out, raat mein ulta, dawn aur dusk par kuch bhi nahin (compensation point). Jarein: hamesha O2 in aur CO2 out, din raat ek jaisa.

🧪 Hissa 2: STEAM Activity

Green Breathing: Unveiling Plant Respiratory Patterns in Day and Night (kitab p123, p124)

Objective: "To recognize how does a plant's exposure to light and darkness impact its gas exchange?"

📖 Principle (kitab ke alfaaz) The increase or decrease in carbon dioxide concentration will be tested in this inquiry by using hydrogen carbonate indicator. It goes from pink or red to yellow when carbon dioxide builds up. The indicator becomes purple when carbon dioxide concentrations fall.

Indicator ka rang yaad karne ki chhoti table:

CO2 ka haalIndicator ka rang
Shuru mein (normal)Pink ya red
CO2 barh gayi (builds up)Yellow
CO2 kam ho gayi (falls)Purple

Procedure (kitab ke poore 5 steps):

  1. "Take three test tubes of 50ml volume and wash them properly with tap water then rinse them with distilled water and finally with hydrogen carbonate indicator (the indicator will change colour if the boiling tube is not clean)."
  2. "Add 30ml hydrogen carbonate indicator solution into each of the three boiling tubes."
  3. "Place equal-sized pieces of Canadian pondweed (Elodea) or Hydrilla or any other locally available aquatic plant in tubes 1 and 2, then use stoppers to close all of the tubes."
  4. "Use a bench lamp to expose tubes 1 and 3 to light, then cover tube 2 with aluminum foil or put it in a dark cabinet or black box (Figure 8.11)."
  5. "Note the colour of each tube's hydrogen carbonate indicator after a period of 24 hours."

Figure 8.11: Experiment to compare gas exchange in plants kept in the dark and in the light. Tasveer mein teen tubes hain. Tube 2 ek kaale dabbe ke andar (andhera) aur us mein pond weed hai. Tube 1 light ke saamne, us mein bhi pond weed hai. Tube 3 light ke saamne magar khaali (sirf indicator). Neeche likha hai "Hydrogencarbonate indicator (pink/red at start)".

🔍 Har tube ka kaam Tube 1 = paudha + roshni (photosynthesis chalegi). Tube 2 = paudha + andhera (sirf respiration). Tube 3 = control, paudha nahin, sirf roshni. Control is liye rakha ke pata chale rang ka farq paudhe ne kiya hai, roshni ne nahin.
📖 Result (kitab ke alfaaz) The indicator in tube 2 (plant in the dark) should turn yellow, the indicator in tube 1 (plant in the light) should turn purple, and the indicator in tube 3 (the control), which was initially pink/red, should not change colour.
TubeHalatRangKyun

📖 Interpretation (kitab ke alfaaz, p123 se p124 tak) In simple terms, hydrogen carbonate indicator helps us see changes in acidity caused by carbon dioxide. When there's more carbon dioxide, it turns orange/red, and when there's less, it becomes purple. An experiment with this indicator and plants showed that in light, plants use more carbon dioxide in photosynthesis than they produce in respiration. In the dark, plants produce carbon dioxide through respiration. A control tube without a plant showed that the plant caused the color change. However, it's noted that the indicator can react to changes in acidity or alkalinity, not just carbon dioxide. Despite this, understanding how leaves work suggests changes in carbon dioxide are more likely.

Reflection: "Suggest students to emphasize the significance of gas exchange in plants for animal life on Earth. Encourage them to contemplate the potential consequences if plants were to become extinct."

⚖️ Hissa 3: Homeostasis aur Excretion

8.4 Homeostasis in Plants (kitab p124)

Homeostasis ka matlab hai paudhe ka apne andar ka balance qaim rakhna, chahe bahar kuch bhi ho raha ho. Garmi bohat ho, paani kam ho, mitti mein namak zyada ho, paudha apne aap ko us ke mutabiq adjust kar leta hai.

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho Homeostasis in plants refers to the adjustment or adaptation of plants to varying degrees of environmental temperatures and availability of water and salts in the soil.

Definition ke teen tukre yaad karo: (1) temperature, (2) paani ki availability, (3) mitti mein salts ki availability.

8.4.1 Importance of Homeostasis (kitab p124)

Kitab ne is ke kayi faide ginwaye hain. Har ek alag point hai, exam mein sab likh sakti ho:

  1. "Homeostasis in plants is essential for their survival, growth, and reproduction."
  2. "Homeostatic mechanisms help plants respond to temperature changes, allowing them to survive in various climates."
  3. "Homeostasis ensures that plants maintain adequate hydration levels." (jism mein paani ki sahi miqdar)
  4. "Homeostasis allows plants to respond to various stressors, such as drought, salinity, pathogens, and herbivores." (khushk sali, namak, beemari wale jarasim, aur ghaas patta khane wale janwar)
  5. "Plants can activate specific defense mechanisms to mitigate damage." (nuqsan kam karne ke liye apna difaa chala lete hain)
  6. "It enables them to adapt to changing environmental conditions and maintain internal stability, ultimately contributing to their ecological success."
🧠 Chaar stressors yaad karne ka tareeqa Drought, Salinity, Pathogens, Herbivores. Yaad rakho: "D S P H" yani Dhoop, Salt, Phupoondi, Hirni. Chaaron alag alag khatra hain.

8.4.2 Excretion in plants (kitab p124)

Janwaron mein excretion ka matlab hota hai gurdon (kidneys) se fazla nikalna. Paudhon mein baat bohat wasee hai: jism se koi bhi cheez nikalna excretion hai.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Unlike animals, excretion in plants is a very broad term, which encompasses the removal of anything from plant body. Plants have a different way of getting rid of waste compared to animals. Plants make various organic and inorganic compounds that they store for different reasons and remove when needed. Following are different ways of excretion in plants.

Kitab paanch tareeqe bayan karti hai. Section III ka Q8 poora yehi hai, is liye paanchon yaad karo:

1. Excretion of carbon dioxide and oxygen (kitab p124)

  • "Carbon dioxide is the waste of respiration which is excreted from plant body at night during gas exchange process."
  • "Similarly, oxygen is the waste of photosynthesis which is excreted in day during the same gas exchange process."

Dhyan do: paudhe ke liye oxygen bhi ek waste hai. Hamare liye zindagi, us ke liye bacha hua maal.

2. Excretion of water (kitab p124)

  • "Water is produced as waste of both photosynthesis and respiration and it is also absorbed from soil."
  • "The excretion of water occurs through the plant body during the process of transpiration."

3. Excretion through leaves (kitab p124)

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Plant cells have large vacuoles that can store useful stuff or waste. Sometimes, these stored substances can build up and form crystals in the vacuoles. Leaves are key players in this process. When the leaves are loaded with large amount pigmented compounds, they turn yellow. Remember, this yellowing is not due to lack of chlorophyll as happens in chlorosis. Such leaves are generally fallen from plants in autumn season. In this way leaves act as organ of excretion, therefore, such leaves are also called excretophores. This is why gardeners like using decomposed autumn leaves as a mineral-rich source for plants.

Asaan alfaaz mein:

  • Plant cells ke bare vacuoles hote hain. In mein kaam ki cheez bhi rakhi ja sakti hai aur fazla bhi.
  • Kabhi kabhi ye jama shuda cheezein crystals bana leti hain.
  • Jab patte pigmented compounds se bhar jate hain to peelay ho jate hain.
  • Ye peelapan chlorosis nahin hai. Chlorosis mein chlorophyll ki kami hoti hai; yahan chlorophyll ki kami nahin, balke fazla jama ho gaya hai.
  • Aise patte autumn (khizan) mein gir jate hain. Patta gira, fazla bhi saath gaya. Is liye patte ko organ of excretion kehte hain aur aise patton ko excretophores.
  • Isi wajah se maali (gardeners) decomposed autumn leaves ko mitti mein daalte hain: ye mineral-rich source hain.
❓ Critical Thinking box (kitab p124): "Can you differentiate between chloroted leaves and excretophores?" Chlorotic leaves: peelay is liye hain ke chlorophyll ban hi nahin paya (mineral ki kami, jaise magnesium ya iron). Ye beemari ki nishani hai.
Excretophores: peelay is liye hain ke un mein pigmented waste compounds bhar gaye. Chlorophyll ki koi kami nahin. Ye normal hai aur ye patte autumn mein gir kar paudhe ka fazla saath le jate hain.
Kitab mein lafz "chloroted" chhapa hai; asal lafz chlorotic hai (chlorosis se). Ye kitab ki printing ki ghalti hai.

4. Excretion through stem and branches (kitab p125)

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Some trees deposit unusual chemicals in their branches and trunks, especially in old xylem that's no longer used for water transport. Ebony trees, for instance, produce very dark wood in their centres. Plant scientists consider these deposits as waste materials. Although, these wastes are not removed but are dumped in one part of the body as we put the trash in waste bin however, it is still present in the home.
  • Kuch darakht apne branches aur trunks mein ajeeb chemicals jama kar lete hain.
  • Khaas tor par us purane xylem mein jo ab paani le jane ka kaam nahin karta.
  • Ebony trees (aabnoos) ki misal: in ke beech ka hissa bohat gehra kaala (very dark wood) ho jata hai.
  • Plant scientists in deposits ko waste materials mante hain.
  • Kitab ki misal: ye fazla jism se nikalta nahin, bas ek hissay mein daal diya jata hai, jaise ghar ka koora waste bin mein daal dete hain magar woh ghar hi mein maujood rehta hai.

5. Excretion through roots (kitab p125)

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Some plants actively release waste compounds into the soil, almost like using them as chemical weapons against other plants competing for resources. Conifer trees are known for this tactic.
  • Kuch paudhe apna fazla mitti mein chhor dete hain.
  • Ye fazla un ke liye chemical weapon ka kaam karta hai un doosre paudhon ke khilaf jo wahan ke resources (paani, minerals) ke liye muqabla kar rahe hain.
  • Conifer trees is chaal (tactic) ke liye mashhoor hain.
✨ Ek line mein Excretion ke paanch raaste: gases (CO2 raat ko, O2 din ko), paani (transpiration se), patte (excretophores), stem aur branches (ebony ki dark wood), aur jarein (conifer ke chemical weapons).

💧 Hissa 4: Osmotic Adjustment

8.4.3 Osmotic adjustment (Osmoregulation) in plants (kitab p125)

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho Osmotic adjustment, also known as osmoregulation, is like a plant's way of maintaining the right balance of water and solutes in its body.

Kitab: "This balance creates three different situations: hypotonic, hypertonic, and isotonic."

Hypotonic Situation (kitab p125)

Kitab: "When a cell is in a watery environment with more water and less stuff dissolved in it (solute), water moves into the cell, making it swell up and firm (we call this 'turgid')."

Bahar paani zyada, solute kam. Paani cell ke andar aaya. Cell phool kar kasa hua (turgid) ho gaya.

Hypertonic Situation (kitab p125)

Kitab: "If a cell is in an environment with less water and more dissolved stuff than it has inside (cell sap), water moves out of the cell, causing it to shrink and become limp (we call this 'flaccid')."

Kitab yahan ek zaroori jumla likhti hai: "Both of these situations, hypotonic and hypertonic, require the plant to make adjustments to keep things in balance."

Ideal Situation (Isotonic) (kitab p125)

Kitab: "The best scenario for a cell is when the water and solute balance inside and outside the cell is just right. In this 'isotonic' situation, there's no net movement of water in or out of the cell. However, this balance is rarely found in a plant's natural environment."

SituationBahar ka maholPaani kidharCell ka haal
HypotonicPaani zyada, solute kamCell ke andarTurgid (phoola aur kasa hua)
HypertonicPaani kam, solute zyadaCell se baharFlaccid (sukra aur dheela)
IsotonicDono taraf barabarKoi net movement nahinNormal, magar qudrat mein bohat kam milta hai
🧠 Hypo aur Hyper ki pakki trick Hypo mein "o" hai, jaise "over water". Bahar paani zyada to cell phoolta hai (turgid).
Hyper mein "er" hai, jaise "dry paani nikal jaye". Bahar solute zyada to cell sukarta hai (flaccid).
Aur yaad rakho: paani hamesha kam solute wali taraf se zyada solute wali taraf jata hai.
🥗 Ghar ki misal Salad ke patton par namak chhirak do: thori der mein patte dheele aur murjhaye ho jate hain. Namak ne bahar solute barha diya (hypertonic), paani cells se nikal gaya, cells flaccid ho gaye. Ab wohi murjhaya patta saaf paani mein daalo: paani wapis andar jayega (hypotonic) aur patta phir kara (turgid) ho jayega.

Osmotic adjustment in different plants groups (kitab p125)

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Plants are categorized into three groups based on their water availability: hydrophytes (live in water-rich environments), mesophytes (thrive where water is moderately available), and xerophytes (adapted to extreme dry conditions). These groups have distinct adaptations:
  • Hydrophytes: paani se bharay mahol mein rehte hain (jaise lotus, water lily).
  • Mesophytes: jahan paani darmiyani ho (aam paudhe, gamle wale paudhe, fasalein).
  • Xerophytes: bohat khushk jaghon ke liye bane hue (jaise cactus, thor).

Kitab ke poore 5 differences (ye table exam mein bohat aati hai):

#FarqHydrophytesMesophytesXerophytes
1Transpiration rateHighest (sab se zyada)DarmiyaniLowest (sab se kam)
2Stomatal placementUpper leaf surface parLower surface parSunken stomata (dhansay hue)
3Stomatal behaviourDin raat khulay rehte hainSirf din mein khulte hainRaat mein khulte hain
4Cuticle thicknessAlmost none (taqreeban nahin)Thinner cuticle (patli)Thickest (sab se moti)
5Water storageZaroorat hi nahinThori siAmple water, inhein succulents kehte hain

Kitab ke apne alfaaz mein point 1: "Transpiration rates vary, with hydrophytes having the highest and xerophytes the lowest." Point 2: "Stomatal placement differs, hydrophytes have them on the upper leaf surface, mesophytes on the lower surface, and xerophytes have sunken stomata." Point 3: "Stomatal behaviour varies, hydrophytes keep stomata open day and night, mesophytes open them during the day, and xerophytes open them at night." Point 4: "Cuticle thickness on leaves differs, with xerophytes having the thickest, mesophytes a thinner cuticle, and hydrophytes almost none." Point 5: "Water storage capacity varies, with xerophytes, known as succulents, storing ample water."

🎯 Pakki trick: sab kuch ek soch se Sirf ek sawal pucho: "is paudhe ko paani bachana hai ya nahin?"
Hydrophyte paani mein khara hai, paani bachane ki zaroorat nahin: stomata upar (taa ke paani ke upar khule rahen), hamesha khulay, cuticle ki zaroorat nahin, transpiration sab se zyada.
Xerophyte ko har boond bachani hai: stomata andar dhansay hue, sirf thandi raat mein khulte hain, cuticle sab se moti, transpiration sab se kam, aur paani jama kar leta hai (succulent).
Mesophyte beech mein hai: har cheez darmiyani.
✨ Ek line mein Hypotonic = turgid, hypertonic = flaccid, isotonic = koi net movement nahin (qudrat mein bohat kam). Hydrophytes paani mein, mesophytes darmiyani, xerophytes khushki mein aur ye succulents kehlate hain.

Q3. Why gardeners like to use autumn leaves in their soil? Paudhon ke cells ke bare vacuoles hote hain jin mein fazla jama hota hai aur kabhi kabhi crystals ban jate hain. Jab patte pigmented compounds se bhar jate hain to peelay ho jate hain aur autumn (khizan) ke mausam mein gir jate hain. Aise patte excretion ka organ hote hain, inhein excretophores kehte hain.
In gire hue patton mein paudhe ke jama kiye hue organic aur inorganic compounds maujood hote hain. Jab ye patte mitti mein decompose (gal sarr) jate hain to un ke minerals mitti mein shamil ho jate hain.
Is liye gardeners decomposed autumn leaves ko mitti mein daalte hain, kyunke ye paudhon ke liye ek "mineral-rich source" hain.
Q5. Write any three osmotic adjustments in hydrophytes. Hydrophytes woh paudhe hain jo water-rich environments mein rehte hain. Un ke osmotic adjustments (koi bhi teen likh do):
  1. Transpiration rate sab se zyada (highest) hoti hai, kyunke paani ki kami ka koi khatra nahin.
  2. Stomata upper leaf surface par hote hain (patte ki upar wali satah par), taa ke wo hawa ke saamne rahen.
  3. Stomata din aur raat dono waqt khulay rehte hain.
  4. Cuticle taqreeban hoti hi nahin (almost none), kyunke paani rokne ki zaroorat nahin.
Paanchwan point (water storage) kitab mein khaas tor par xerophytes ke liye likha hai, hydrophytes ke liye nahin. Is liye upar wale chaar mein se koi teen chuno.
Q6. What is the pattern of gas exchange between plant and environment at the time of dawn and dusk? Dawn (subah saweray) aur dusk (shaam dhalte) ke waqt light intensity kam hoti hai. Is wajah se photosynthesis aur respiration ki rates barabar ho jati hain.
  • Respiration se bani hui carbon dioxide theek utni hi hoti hai jitni photosynthesis ko chahiye.
  • Photosynthesis se nikli hui oxygen theek utni hi hoti hai jitni respiration istemal kar leti hai.
Nateeja: mahol ke saath gas ka koi net exchange nahin hota. Na CO2 andar aati hai, na O2 bahar jati hai. Is halat ko "compensation point of photosynthesis" kehte hain.

Section III: Extensive Answer Questions

Q7. Describe the process of gaseous exchange in plants. Gas exchange kya hai
Gas exchange woh respiration hai jo organismic level par hoti hai, yani poora organism mahol ke saath gases ka len den karta hai. Maqsad ye hai ke organism ko oxygen mile jo cellular respiration ke liye chahiye (jahan complex organic compounds toote hain), aur jo carbon dioxide banti hai woh bahar nikal jaye.

Kahan aur kaise
Paudhe ka har cell apni gas exchange khud karta hai, apni zaroorat ke mutabiq. Ye exchange khaas tor par do openings se hota hai: stomata (patton ke chhote sooraakh) aur lenticels (tanon ke chhote sooraakh). Ye sab diffusion ke amal se hota hai.

1. Patton mein pattern (photosynthetic parts)
Paudhe photosynthetic autotrophs hain, is liye patton mein din aur raat ka pattern alag hota hai.
In daytimeIn nighttime
InhaleCarbon dioxide (CO2)Oxygen (O2)
ExhaleOxygen (O2)Carbon dioxide (CO2)
Din mein: paudha photosynthesis aur respiration dono kar raha hota hai. Photosynthesis ki rate light intensity par depend karti hai aur aam tor par respiration se zyada hoti hai. Is liye photosynthesis ko respiration se milne wali CO2 kaafi nahin hoti aur paudha mahol se extra CO2 leta hai. Doosri taraf photosynthesis respiration ki zaroorat se zyada O2 banati hai, is liye paudha fazil O2 release kar deta hai.
Raat mein: andhere mein photosynthesis hoti hi nahin, is liye paudhe janwaron jaise behave karte hain: O2 absorb aur CO2 release.
Dawn aur dusk par: light intensity kam hone se photosynthesis aur respiration ki rates barabar ho jati hain. Respiration ki CO2 photosynthesis ke liye kaafi hoti hai aur photosynthesis ki O2 respiration istemal kar leti hai. Mahol ke saath koi net exchange nahin hota, aur isay compensation point of photosynthesis kehte hain.

2. Jaron mein pattern (non-photosynthetic parts)
Jarein soil air ke saath gas exchange karti hain, root epidermal cells ke zariye. Wo oxygen absorb karti hain aur carbon dioxide release karti hain, kyunke jaron mein photosynthesis bilkul nahin hoti. Ye pattern din aur raat, dono waqt ek jaisa rehta hai.
Q8. Describe the mechanism of adaptations of plants for the excretion. Paudhon mein excretion kya hai
Janwaron ke bar aks, paudhon mein excretion ek bohat wasee (very broad) lafz hai jis ka matlab hai paudhe ke jism se kisi bhi cheez ka nikalna. Paudhon ka fazla nikalne ka tareeqa janwaron se alag hai. Paudhe tarah tarah ke organic aur inorganic compounds banate hain, unhein mukhtalif wajuhat se jama karte hain, aur zaroorat par nikal dete hain. Kitab ke mutabiq excretion ke paanch raaste hain:

1. Excretion of carbon dioxide and oxygen
Carbon dioxide respiration ka waste hai, jo raat ke waqt gas exchange ke amal mein jism se bahar nikaali jati hai. Isi tarah oxygen photosynthesis ka waste hai, jo din mein usi gas exchange ke amal se bahar nikaali jati hai.

2. Excretion of water
Paani photosynthesis aur respiration dono ka waste hai, aur ye mitti se absorb bhi hota hai. Paani ka excretion paudhe ke jism se transpiration ke amal ke doran hota hai.

3. Excretion through leaves
Plant cells ke bare vacuoles hote hain jo kaam ki cheezein ya waste jama kar sakte hain. Kabhi kabhi ye jama shuda cheezein barh kar vacuoles mein crystals bana leti hain. Is amal mein patte sab se aham hain. Jab patte pigmented compounds ki bari miqdar se bhar jate hain to woh peelay ho jate hain. Ye peelapan chlorosis ki tarah chlorophyll ki kami se nahin hota. Aise patte aam tor par autumn ke mausam mein gir jate hain. Is tarah patte organ of excretion ban jate hain, is liye aise patton ko excretophores kehte hain. Yehi wajah hai ke gardeners decomposed autumn leaves ko paudhon ke liye mineral-rich source ke tor par istemal karna pasand karte hain.

4. Excretion through stem and branches
Kuch darakht apni branches aur trunks mein ghair maamooli chemicals jama kar lete hain, khaas tor par us purane xylem mein jo ab paani le jane ke kaam nahin aata. Misal ke tor par ebony trees apne beech wale hisse mein bohat gehri kaali lakri (very dark wood) banate hain. Plant scientists in deposits ko waste materials samajhte hain. Ye waste jism se nikalta nahin, bas jism ke ek hisse mein daal diya jata hai, bilkul jaise hum koora waste bin mein daal dete hain magar woh ghar hi mein maujood rehta hai.

5. Excretion through roots
Kuch paudhe apne waste compounds ko mitti mein chhor dete hain, taqreeban is tarah jaise unhein chemical weapons ke tor par un doosre paudhon ke khilaf istemal kar rahe hon jo resources ke liye muqabla kar rahe hain. Conifer trees is chaal ke liye mashhoor hain.
Q9. Explain osmotic adjustment in plants. Tareef
Osmotic adjustment, jise osmoregulation bhi kehte hain, paudhe ka apne jism mein paani aur solutes ka sahi balance qaim rakhne ka tareeqa hai. Is balance se teen halatein banti hain: hypotonic, hypertonic aur isotonic.

1. Hypotonic situation
Jab cell aise paani wale mahol mein ho jahan paani zyada aur solute kam ho, to paani cell ke andar chala jata hai. Cell phool kar sakht aur kasa hua ho jata hai, is halat ko turgid kehte hain.

2. Hypertonic situation
Agar cell aise mahol mein ho jahan cell sap ke muqable mein paani kam aur dissolved stuff zyada ho, to paani cell se bahar nikal jata hai. Cell sukar kar dheela par jata hai, is halat ko flaccid kehte hain.
Ye dono halatein (hypotonic aur hypertonic) paudhe se taqaza karti hain ke woh adjustments kare taa ke cheezein balance mein rahen.

3. Ideal situation (Isotonic)
Cell ke liye behtareen soorat ye hai ke cell ke andar aur bahar paani aur solute ka balance bilkul theek ho. Is isotonic halat mein cell ke andar ya bahar paani ki koi net movement nahin hoti. Lekin ye balance paudhe ke qudrati mahol mein bohat kam milta hai.

Osmotic adjustment in different plants groups
Paani ki availability ke lihaz se paudhon ke teen groups hain: hydrophytes (paani se bharay mahol mein rehne wale), mesophytes (jahan paani darmiyani miqdar mein ho) aur xerophytes (bohat khushk halat ke liye adapted). In ki adaptations alag alag hain:
