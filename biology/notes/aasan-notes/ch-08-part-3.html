💨 Hissa 1: Transpiration

8.2.3 Transpiration (kitab p117)

Socho: tum ne gamle (pot) mein subah ek glass pani daala. Sham tak wo pani gaya kahan? Thora sa plant ne apne kaam mein lagaya, lekin taqreeban saara pani patton se water vapour ban kar hawa mein ur gaya. Bas yehi transpiration hai.

Kitab ke alfaaz (p117): "You have learnt that plants absorb water from the soil by the roots. This absorbed water moves in the areal parts of the plant from where the most of this water (approx. 99%) has been lost in the form of vapours into the atmosphere. This loss is called transpiration."

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho Transpiration is the loss of the most of the absorbed water (approx. 99%) in the form of vapours into the atmosphere from the areal (aerial) parts of the plant.

Ab ye 99% pani nikalta kahan kahan se hai? Kitab teen raste batati hai.

QisamKitna paniKahan se, kitab ke alfaaz mein
Stomatal transpiration90% of this loss"through the stomata present mainly in the leaves and some other aerial parts of the plant"
Cuticular transpiration7 to 9% of this absorbed water"lost from general body surface through the cuticle"
Lenticular transpirationless than 3% (a very little amount)"through lenticels which are scar like regions (with ruptured epidermis and loosely packed underlying cortical cells) present in the stem"
🧠 Pakki trick: teen numbers gadbad na karna 99% = kitna absorbed water zaaya hota hai (total loss).
90% = is loss ka kitna hissa stomata se jata hai.
7 to 9% = cuticle se. 3% se kam = lenticels se.
Yaad karne ka tareeqa: Stomata sab se zyada, Cuticle darmiyana, Lenticel sab se kam. Yani S > C > L.
🤔 Critical Thinking (kitab p117) "Does a submerged aquatic plant perform transpiration."
Kitab is ka jawab nahin deti, ye sirf sochne ke liye hai. Sochne ka raasta: transpiration ka matlab hai pani ka water vapour ban kar hawa mein jana. Jo plant poora pani ke andar doobi hui hai, us ke paas hawa hai hi nahin ke vapours bana kar bhejay.

Transpiration ke faide (kitab p117, lafz ba lafz): "Transpiration is a vital process for the plant as it not only provides cooling to the plant but also help to absorb the water and dissolved minerals and to move them in upward direction to the leaves. Therefore, without transpiration plants are unable to survive."

Aur nuqsan? Kitab: "On the other hand, transpiration is generally regarded as disadvantageous as it shows great loss of absorbed water so, an unchecked excessive transpiration can lead to the wilting (loss of turgor) and ultimately death of the plant."

🤔 Critical Thinking (kitab p117) "Why transpiration is sometimes called necessary evil."
Is ka poora jawab aakhir wale exercise hisse mein hai (Section II, sawal 1). Choti si baat: necessary kyunke is ke baghair plant zinda nahin reh sakta, evil kyunke is mein 99% pani zaaya ho jata hai.
🏠 Ghar ki misal Garmi mein jab tumhein paseena aata hai to jism thanda ho jata hai, lekin saath hi pani bhi kam ho jata hai, is liye pyaas lagti hai. Plant ke liye transpiration bilkul paseene jaisa hai: thanda bhi karta hai, aur pani bhi le jata hai. Aur jaise paseene ke baad pani peena parta hai, waise hi transpiration ki kheench se jar se naya pani upar aata hai.

Relation of transpiration with leaf surface area and Stomatal opening and closing (kitab p117)

Kitab: "As you have learnt that transpiration is mainly occurs through the stomata, which are present on the leaf surface. So, the plants having large surface area of the leaves having more stomata show greater rate of transpiration as compared to the plants having narrow and reduced surfaces of the leaves having less stomata."

Aasan lafzon mein: patta jitna bara, us par stomata utne zyada, aur transpiration utni zyada. Chhota aur patla patta (jaise cactus ka kaanta) matlab kam stomata, matlab kam transpiration.

Aur waqt ka farq? Kitab: "Similarly, more transpiration is generally observed in date time than night as the stomata are widely open during day and nearly close at night."

⚠️ Kitab ki chhapai ki ghalti Yahan kitab ne "date time" likha hai. Asal lafz "day time" hai (din ka waqt). Tum exam mein day time hi likhna.
Isi page par kitab ne "areal parts" bhi likha hai. Sahi lafz "aerial parts" hai, yani plant ke wo hisse jo hawa mein hain (tana aur patte).
🤔 Critical Thinking (kitab p117) "Can you think that the shape and the size of the leaves would be an adaptation of the plants in different habitats such as aquatic and terrestrial."
Sochne ka raasta: registan ke plants ke patte chhote, sakht ya kaanton jaise hote hain taa ke pani kam nikle. Pani ke plants ke patte chaure hote hain kyunke unhein pani bachane ki fikr nahin.
🔹 Ek line mein (Hissa 1) Transpiration = aerial parts se absorbed pani (approx 99%) ka water vapour ban kar nikalna. 90% stomata se, 7 se 9% cuticle se, 3% se kam lenticels se. Bara patta aur khuley stomata = zyada transpiration, aur din mein raat se zyada.

🌡️ Hissa 2: Factors affecting the rate of transpiration

8.2.4 Factors affecting the rate of transpiration (kitab p118)

Kitab: "The rate of transpiration is influenced by several environmental factors like temperature, wind, and humidity etc. The rate of translation can be measured by using a simple apparatus called the potometer which is shown in the diagram. Students under the guidance of their teachers should plan simple experimental activities to investigate the effect of these factors on the rate of transpiration using the photometer."

⚠️ Kitab ki chhapai ki do ghaltiyan, isi paragraph mein 1. "The rate of translation can be measured": yahan transpiration hona chahiye tha. Translation ka biology mein matlab bilkul aur hai (protein banna).
2. "using the photometer": sahi lafz potometer hai. Photometer roshni naapne wala aala hota hai; potometer transpiration ki rate naapta hai. Exam mein hamesha potometer likhna.
🔬 Figure: 8.7 Investigation of rate of transpiration by using [potometer] Tasveer ke labels: cut shoot (kaati hui tehni), reservoir (pani ka zakheera), air bubble (hawa ka bulbula), capillary tube (bohat patli naali), volume scale (volume naapne wali patti), beaker of water.
Chalta kaise hai: cut shoot jitna pani kheenchti hai, capillary tube mein air bubble utna aage sarakta hai. Bubble ka safar volume scale par parh lo, transpiration ki rate maloom.
Kitab mein is caption ka aakhri lafz page ke kinaray par kat gaya hai; jumla "by using potometer" banta hai.

Effect of temperature (kitab p118)

Kitab: "On a sunny day with strong sunlight, the air temperature rises, and this increase in temperature lowers the humidity in the air. As a result, more water evaporates from the surfaces of plant mesophyll cells, which leads to a higher rate of transpiration. For every 10°C increase in temperature, the rate of transpiration roughly doubles. However, when the environmental temperature becomes very high, around 40 to 45°C, it causes the stomata on plant leaves to close. This closure helps the plant conserve its much needed water because excessive loss can be detrimental."

Aur agar garmi lambi chal jaye? Kitab: "If these higher temperatures persist for an extended period and the soil doesn't have enough water, the plants may start to wilt and could eventually die."

🧠 Pakki trick: temperature ka ulta mor Temperature barhne se rate barhti hai (har 10°C par taqreeban double), lekin 40 se 45°C par baat ulti ho jati hai: stomata band ho jate hain aur rate gir jati hai.
Plant sochta hai: "itni garmi mein agar main darwaze khule rakhoon to saara pani khatam. Behtar hai darwaze band kar loon."

Effect of Wind (kitab p118)

Kitab: "Wind is moving air, and it speeds up the diffusion of water molecules. This leads to a higher rate of evaporation from the surfaces of mesophyll cells. In contrast, when the air is calm and still, the movement of water molecules (diffusion) slows down, resulting in a decreased rate of transpiration."

🌬️ Ghar ki misal Geeley kapre chhat par kyun sukhate hain? Kyunke wahan hawa chalti hai. Hawa geeli hawa ko hata kar sookhi hawa le aati hai, is liye pani jaldi urta hai. Band kamre mein wohi kapra der se sukhta hai. Patta bhi bilkul aisa hi hai.

Effect of Humidity (kitab p119)

Kitab: "Humidity is the percentage of water vapours in the air. In dry air (low humidity), water molecules diffuse more rapidly from the surfaces of mesophyll cells, air spaces, and through stomata to the outside of the leaf. This results in more water being lost, which increases the rate of transpiration. Conversely, in humid air, the diffusion rate slows down, leading to a significant decrease in the rate of transpiration."

Aur kitab ka agla jumla ek sawal hai: "Similarly, can you predict the effect of other factors like sun light, CO2 concentration and availability of soil water on the rate of transpiration."

⬆️ Hissa 3: Ascent of Sap aur TACT Theory

8.2.4 Transport of water and salts within plant body (Ascent of Sap) (kitab p119)

⚠️ Number ki ghalti, ghabrana nahin Kitab ne do sections ko 8.2.4 ka number de diya hai: p118 wala "Factors affecting the rate of transpiration" bhi 8.2.4, aur p119 wala "Transport of water and salts within plant body (Ascent of Sap)" bhi 8.2.4. Ye sirf chhapai ki ghalti hai. Tum headings ke naam yaad rakho, numbers nahin.

Kitab: "Root epidermal cells absorb water and minerals from the soil. These substances are then transported from the roots to the leaves. This upward movement of water and dissolved minerals through the xylem tissue is known as the ascent of sap. This process requires a passage and a significant amount of force because it occurs against the force of gravity, especially in tall plants. It is now quite evident that passage of ascent of sap is the xylem tissue and the most widely accepted theory that explains the force responsible for ascent of sap is called the TACT theory."

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho The upward movement of water and dissolved minerals through the xylem tissue is known as the ascent of sap.
🌳 Soch kar dekho Ek 20 foot ooncha darakht. Us ki jar se pani upar sab se oonche patte tak jata hai. Koi pump nahin, koi motor nahin, koi bijli nahin. Phir bhi gravity ke khilaf pani upar chalta rehta hai. Kitab kehti hai is ke liye do cheezein chahiyein: ek raasta (passage) aur ek taaqat (force). Raasta = xylem. Taaqat = TACT.

Passage of Ascent of Sap (kitab p119)

Kitab: "Xylem is a complex and permanent tissue which act as passage for ascent of sap. Two type of xylem cells: Tracheids and Vessel are very important. Tracheids are long, dead cells with pointed ends and Xylem vessels are long, dead, and thick-walled tube-like structures formed by the fusion of vessel cells (vessel elements) placed end to end. Both are essential for water and mineral transport as well as structural support."

Xylem ke do ahem cellsKitab kya kehti hai
TracheidsLong, dead cells with pointed ends
Vessels (vessel elements)Long, dead, thick-walled tube-like structures, banti hain vessel cells ke fusion se jo end to end laggi hoti hain
Dono ka kaamWater aur mineral transport, aur saath hi structural support
🧠 Dhyan do: ye cells MARE huey hain Tracheids aur vessels dono dead cells hain. Ye achhi baat hai! Andar koi cytoplasm nahin, koi nucleus nahin, bas khaali naali. Jaise plastic pipe. Pani bila rukawat guzar jata hai. Is ke bar aks phloem ke sieve tubes zinda hote hain.

TACT Theory (kitab p119, p120)

Kitab: "According to the TACT theory, four factors work together to make a force that move water and minerals up a plant. These factors are Transpiration pull, Adhesion, Cohesion, and Tension, forming the acronym TACT."

T + A + C + T = Transpiration pull, Adhesion, Cohesion, Tension

Ab kitab char factors ko alag alag kholti hai (p120):

i) Transpiration Pull: "When stomata (tiny openings) on leaves are open, water evaporates from the leaves into the air, creating a pulling force known as transpiration. This pull makes water move from the roots to the leaves through the xylem tissue."

ii) Adhesion: "Adhesion is the attraction between water molecules and other substances. Water is strongly attracted to the walls of the xylem cells because both water and cellulose (in cell walls) are polar molecules. This adhesion helps water move upward in the plant against gravity. It also keeps water in the xylem when transpiration is not happening."

iii) Cohesion and Tension: "Cohesion is the attraction between nearby water molecules, which is possible because water is a polar molecule. Tension refers to the tension created in the water column. These forces help hold the water molecules together in a continuous column within the xylem."

🧠 Adhesion aur Cohesion mein farq (MCQ ka favourite) Cohesion = pani ka molecule doosre pani ke molecule se chipakna. (Co = companion, apna saathi.)
Adhesion = pani ka molecule kisi aur cheez se chipakna, jaise xylem ki deewar. (Adhesive = gond, jo doosri cheez par lagta hai.)
Dono mumkin isi liye hain ke water ek polar molecule hai.

How TACT Force Works (kitab p120)

Kitab: "Imagine the column of water in the xylem as strong as a steel wire. Hydrogen bonds among water molecules create cohesion, holding the water molecules together like a string. As long as transpiration continues (water evaporating from leaves), the string of water remains tense and is pulled upward as a single unit. This bulk flow of water to the top of the plant is driven by solar energy, as it's the evaporation from leaves that causes the transpiration pull."

🧵 Dhaage wali misal Socho pani ke saare molecules ek dhaaga ban gaye hain, ek doosre ka haath pakre huey (cohesion, hydrogen bonds ki wajah se). Upar patte se jab ek molecule urta hai (transpiration) to wo apne peechay waale ko kheench leta hai, wo agle ko, aur kheench poori jar tak chali jati hai. Dhaaga kabhi tootta nahin, is liye kitab kehti hai wo steel wire jitna mazboot hai. Aur deewar se chipke rehne ki wajah se (adhesion) dhaaga phisal kar neeche bhi nahin girta.
⭐ Exam ki khaas line "This bulk flow of water to the top of the plant is driven by solar energy." Yani asal engine sooraj hai. Sooraj ki garmi se patte se pani urta hai, us se transpiration pull banti hai, aur usi se poora pani upar aata hai. Plant apni koi ATP kharch nahin karta.

1. Upar, patte mein (TRANSPIRATION): labels hain Xylem sap, Leaf, Mesophyll cells, Stoma, Water molecule, Air. Pani ke molecules stoma se bahar hawa mein ja rahe hain.
2. Beech mein, tane mein (COHESION AND ADHESION IN THE XYLEM): labels hain Xylem cells, Stem, Adhesion, Cell Wall, aur Cohesion, by hydrogen bonding. Bari tasveer mein dikhaya gaya hai ke pani ke gole ek doosre se (cohesion) aur cell wall se (adhesion) jure huey hain.
3. Neeche, jar mein (Water UPTAKE FROM SOIL): labels hain Water molecule, Root hair, Soil, Water, Root.
🔹 Ek line mein (Hissa 3) Ascent of sap = pani aur ghule huey minerals ka xylem ke zariye jar se patton tak upar jana, gravity ke khilaf. Raasta = xylem (tracheids + vessels, dono dead). Taaqat = TACT (Transpiration pull, Adhesion, Cohesion, Tension), aur is bulk flow ka engine solar energy hai.

🍬 Hissa 4: Translocation

8.2.5 Transport of organic solute within plant body (kitab p120)

Ab ulti taraf. Pani to jar se patton tak gaya. Lekin patton ne photosynthesis se jo khana banaya, wo jar, phal aur poore plant tak kaise pohanchay? Is ka naam hai translocation.

Kitab: "The transport of prepared food (organic solutes) to different parts of the plant through the phloem tissue is translocation."

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho The transport of prepared food (organic solutes) to different parts of the plant through the phloem tissue is translocation.

Passage of Translocation (kitab p120, p121)

Kitab: "It is evident that translocation occurs through phloem tissues which are complex and permanent plant. Sieve tube elements and companion cells are important components of phloem."

Kitab (p121): "Sieve Tubes are long tube-like structures that transport organic solutes (like sucrose) throughout the plant. Since sieve tube elements lack nuclei, companion cells next to them control their functions. Together, sieve elements and companion cells act as conducting channels for moving organic solutes."

⚠️ Chhoti si chhapai ki ghalti Kitab likhti hai "phloem tissues which are complex and permanent plant". Yahan lafz "permanent tissue" hona chahiye tha, jaise xylem ke liye likha gaya hai. Exam mein likho: phloem is a complex and permanent tissue.
🧠 Pakki trick: sieve tube ka nucleus nahin hota Sieve tube zinda hai lekin us ka nucleus nahin hota. To phir wo chalta kaise hai? Us ke bilkul saath jo companion cell hai, wohi us ke kaam control karta hai.
Misal: sieve tube ek gaari hai jis mein steering nahin, aur companion cell baghal mein baitha driver hai jo usay chala raha hai. Ye baat MCQ mein bohat aati hai.

Direction of Translocation (kitab p121)

Kitab: "This movement is not just up or down; it goes in various directions. We usually say that translocation goes from a source to a sink. A source supplies food (like leaves making food), and a sink uses food (like roots or storage organs). Leaves are sources, fruits are sinks, and stems and roots can act as both."

Source (khana deta hai)Sink (khana kharch karta hai)Dono ban sakte hain
Leaves (jo food banate hain)Fruits, roots, storage organsStems aur roots
🥔 Aloo wali misal Garmiyon mein patte khana banate hain (source) aur wo khana neeche aloo mein jama ho jata hai (sink). Lekin jab naya plant ugna shuru karta hai to wohi aloo apna jama shuda khana bahar bhejta hai, yani ab aloo source ban gaya. Isi liye kitab kehti hai stems aur roots dono kaam kar sakte hain.

Composition of Translocating Fluid (Phloem Sap) (kitab p121)

Kitab: "Phloem sap contains about 10 to 25% dry matter. Most of this dry matter is sucrose (a kind of sugar), with some other organic compounds."

📖 Yaad karne wala number Phloem sap = 10 to 25% dry matter, aur is dry matter ka zyada hissa sucrose hai, baqi kuch doosray organic compounds.
Yani phloem sap ka baqi 75 se 90% hissa pani hai.

Mechanism of Translocation (Pressure Flow or Mass Flow Theory) (kitab p121)

Kitab: "The most accepted idea for how organic solutes moves in plants is the pressure flow or mass flow theory. Here's how it works:"

  1. "Sugars produced in source regions, like leaves or storage areas, are loaded into the phloem's sieve tube elements by companion cells."
  2. "This active transport raises the sugar concentration and reduces the water concentration in the phloem. This makes the phloem cells more sweet."
  3. "Water moves into the phloem through osmosis from nearby xylem cells, increasing hydrostatic pressure (pressure from water) in the phloem cells. This pressure pushes the sugary solution away from the source (usually a leaf)."
  4. "The pressure difference, from the high-pressure source to the lower-pressure sink, causes translocation. It's like water being pushed through a hose."
  5. "When the solution reaches a sink, like the roots, root cells actively absorb the organic solutes. This makes the phloem sap less sweet."
  6. "The loss of solutes in phloem sap increases solute potential and water potential, causing water to flow back into the xylem tubes."

Kitab ka akhri jumla: "So, it's like a system of moving food and water around the plant, driven by differences in pressure and the needs of different plant parts."

🚿 Hose pipe wali misal (kitab ki apni misal) Bagh mein hose pipe socho. Ek sire par nalka khol do, pressure banta hai aur pani doosray sire tak khud chala jata hai. Phloem mein "nalka" source hai: wahan sugar bharne se pani osmosis se andar aata hai aur pressure banta hai. Sink par sugar nikal jati hai to pressure kam ho jata hai. Pressure zyada se pressure kam ki taraf, bas yehi pressure flow hai.
🧠 Loading aur unloading dono mein ATP lagti hai Figure 8.9 saaf likhta hai: source par "Sucrose loaded (requires ATP)" aur sink par "Sucrose unloaded (requires ATP)".
Bara farq yaad rakho: Ascent of sap (xylem) mein plant ki apni energy kharch nahin hoti, wo solar energy se chalta hai. Translocation (phloem) mein ATP kharch hoti hai, is liye ye active process hai.
🖼️ Figure: 8.9 Pressure flow Mechanism for Phloem Transport Kitab ka caption: "Sugar is actively loaded into the sieve tube at the source. As a result, water moves into the sieve tubes by osmosis. At the sink, the sugar is actively unloaded, and water leaves the sieve tube by osmosis."
Tasveer ke labels: SOURCE leaf cells, upar likha hai "At source: Sucrose loaded (requires ATP), Water moves in osmotically". Beech mein Sieve tube running through the length of the plant aur Companion cells. Neeche SINK root cells, aur wahan likha hai "Sucrose unloaded (requires ATP), Water moves out osmotically".
⚠️ Figure 8.9 ki label ghalti Tasveer mein neeche wala label bhi "At source:" likha hua hai, halanke wahan sucrose unload ho rahi hai aur pani bahar nikal raha hai. Wahan "At sink:" hona chahiye tha.
Agar exam mein ye diagram banao to neeche SINK hi likhna.
🔹 Ek line mein (Hissa 4) Translocation = tayyar khana (organic solutes) phloem ke zariye poore plant mein jana, source se sink tak, har taraf. Phloem = sieve tubes (nucleus ke baghair) + companion cells (jo unhein control karte hain). Phloem sap mein 10 se 25% dry matter, zyada tar sucrose. Chalane wali cheez = pressure difference (pressure flow ya mass flow theory), aur loading aur unloading dono ATP maangti hain.

MCQ 8. What drives the translocation of organic solutes in plants? A) Differences in sugar concentration    (B) Differences in leaf size
B) Differences in root structure    (D) Differences in stem length
Jawab: (A) Differences in sugar concentration

Leaf size, root structure aur stem length ka is se koi taluq nahin.

Section II: Short Answer Questions (kitab p127, p128)

Q1. Why is transpiration called necessary evil? Transpiration ko necessary evil (aisi buri cheez jo zaroori bhi hai) is liye kaha jata hai ke us ke faide aur nuqsan dono bohat bare hain.

Necessary (zaroori) kyun:
  • It provides cooling to the plant.
  • It helps the plant to absorb water and dissolved minerals from the soil.
  • It helps to move water and minerals in the upward direction to the leaves (ascent of sap ki transpiration pull isi se banti hai).
  • Kitab ke alfaaz: "without transpiration plants are unable to survive."
Evil (nuqsan deh) kyun:
  • Is mein absorbed water ka bohat bara hissa (approx. 99%) zaaya ho jata hai, is liye "transpiration is generally regarded as disadvantageous as it shows great loss of absorbed water".
  • Unchecked excessive transpiration se plant wilting (loss of turgor) ka shikar ho jata hai aur ultimately death of the plant ho sakti hai.
Yani plant is ke baghair zinda nahin reh sakta, aur is ke saath us ka 99% pani chala jata hai. Isi liye ise necessary evil kehte hain.
Q2. What is the composition of translocating flood? Pehle ek baat: kitab ne yahan "flood" chhaap diya hai. Asal lafz "fluid" hai. Sawal ka matlab hai: translocating fluid, yani phloem sap, kis cheez se bana hota hai? Kitab ke page 121 par heading hi ye hai: "Composition of Translocating Fluid (Phloem Sap)".

Jawab:
  • Translocating fluid ko phloem sap kehte hain, kyunke ye phloem ke sieve tubes mein behta hai.
  • Phloem sap mein about 10 to 25% dry matter hota hai.
  • Most of this dry matter is sucrose (a kind of sugar).
  • Is ke ilawa kuch doosre organic compounds bhi hote hain.
  • Baqi hissa (taqreeban 75 se 90%) pani hai, jo xylem se osmosis ke zariye phloem mein aata hai.
Ek line mein: phloem sap = 10 to 25% dry matter, jis ka zyada tar hissa sucrose hai, saath mein kuch aur organic compounds, aur baqi pani.
Q4. What is the effect of temperature on the rate of transportation? Pehle ek baat: kitab ne yahan "transportation" chhaap diya hai. Sawal asal mein transpiration ka hai (kitab p118 ki heading "Effect of temperature", jo transpiration ke factors mein hai). Jawab transpiration ka hi likhna hai.

Jawab:
  • Tez dhoop wale din air temperature barhta hai, aur temperature barhne se hawa ki humidity kam ho jati hai.
  • Is ki wajah se mesophyll cells ki surfaces se zyada pani evaporate hota hai, yani rate of transpiration barh jati hai.
  • Kitab ka pakka number: "For every 10°C increase in temperature, the rate of transpiration roughly doubles."
  • Lekin jab temperature bohat zyada ho jaye, around 40 to 45°C, to plant ke patton ke stomata band ho jate hain. Is se rate of transpiration gir jati hai. Ye closure plant ko apna zaroori pani bachane mein madad deta hai, kyunke zyada loss us ke liye detrimental (nuqsan deh) hai.
  • Agar ye ooncha temperature lambe arse tak rahe aur mitti mein pani kaafi na ho, to plants wilt karne lagte hain aur aakhir kaar mar bhi sakte hain.
Khulasa: temperature barhne se rate barhti hai (har 10°C par taqreeban double), magar 40 se 45°C par stomata band ho jane se rate kam ho jati hai.
Q8. Define following terms (ii, iii, viii, ix) (ii) Transpiration: The loss of most of the absorbed water (approx. 99%) in the form of vapours into the atmosphere from the aerial parts of the plant is called transpiration. Is ka 90% hissa stomata se (stomatal transpiration), 7 to 9% cuticle se (cuticular transpiration) aur 3% se kam lenticels se (lenticular transpiration) hota hai.

(iii) Translocation: The transport of prepared food (organic solutes) to different parts of the plant through the phloem tissue is called translocation. Ye source se sink ki taraf hoti hai.

(viii) Adhesion: Adhesion is the attraction between water molecules and other substances. Xylem mein pani ke molecules xylem cells ki deewaron se kheenche jate hain, kyunke water aur cellulose dono polar molecules hain. Ye adhesion pani ko gravity ke khilaf upar chalne mein madad deti hai aur jab transpiration nahin ho rahi hoti tab pani ko xylem ke andar rokay rakhti hai.

(ix) Cohesion: Cohesion is the attraction between nearby water molecules, which is possible because water is a polar molecule. Ye hydrogen bonds ki wajah se hoti hai aur pani ke molecules ko xylem ke andar ek continuous column (lagatar lambi lari) ki soorat mein jore rakhti hai.

Q4. Describe transpiration and relate this process with the cell surface and stomatal opening and closing. 1. Transpiration kya hai: Plants roots ke zariye mitti se pani absorb karte hain. Ye absorbed water plant ke aerial parts mein jata hai aur wahan se is ka zyada tar hissa (approx. 99%) vapours ki shakal mein atmosphere mein chala jata hai. Isi loss ko transpiration kehte hain.

2. Transpiration ki teen qismein:
  • Stomatal transpiration: is loss ka 90%, jo stomata se hota hai. Stomata zyada tar leaves mein aur kuch doosre aerial parts mein hote hain.
  • Cuticular transpiration: absorbed water ka 7 to 9%, jo general body surface se cuticle ke zariye nikalta hai.
  • Lenticular transpiration: 3% se kam, jo lenticels se nikalta hai. Lenticels scar like regions hain (ruptured epidermis aur loosely packed underlying cortical cells ke saath) jo stem par hote hain.
3. Transpiration ki ahmiyat: ye plant ke liye ek vital process hai. Ye plant ko cooling deta hai, water aur dissolved minerals absorb karne mein madad deta hai, aur unhein upward direction mein leaves tak pohanchata hai. Is ke baghair plants zinda nahin reh sakte. Doosri taraf ise disadvantageous bhi kaha jata hai kyunke is mein absorbed water ka bohat bara hissa zaaya hota hai, aur unchecked excessive transpiration se plant mein wilting (loss of turgor) aur aakhir kaar death ho sakti hai. Isi liye ise necessary evil kaha jata hai.

4. Leaf surface (cell surface) ke saath rishta: transpiration zyada tar stomata se hoti hai, aur stomata leaf surface par hote hain. Is liye:
  • Jin plants ke patton ka surface area bara hota hai, un par stomata zyada hote hain, aur wo greater rate of transpiration dikhate hain.
  • Jin plants ke patton ki surfaces narrow aur reduced hoti hain, un par stomata kam hote hain, aur un ki transpiration ki rate kam hoti hai.
  • Is ke ilawa pani mesophyll cells ki surfaces se evaporate ho kar air spaces mein aata hai aur phir stomata se bahar jata hai, yani cell surface jitni zyada exposed ho, evaporation utna zyada.
5. Stomatal opening aur closing ke saath rishta:
  • Din mein stomata widely open hote hain, is liye din mein transpiration raat se zyada hoti hai.
  • Raat ko stomata nearly close ho jate hain, is liye transpiration bohat kam ho jati hai.
  • Bohat zyada garmi (around 40 to 45°C) mein bhi stomata band ho jate hain taa ke plant apna zaroori pani bacha sake, aur us waqt bhi transpiration gir jati hai.
Khulasa: transpiration ka darwaza stomata hai. Patta bara aur stomata zyada = transpiration zyada. Stomata khule (din) = transpiration zyada; stomata band (raat, ya bohat zyada garmi) = transpiration kam.
Q5. Describe temperature, wind and humidity as factors affecting the rate of transpiration. Transpiration ki rate kai environmental factors se badalti hai, jaise temperature, wind aur humidity. Ye rate ek simple apparatus potometer se naapi jati hai, jis mein capillary tube ke andar air bubble ki harkat ko volume scale par parha jata hai (Figure 8.7).

1. Effect of temperature:
  • Tez dhoop wale din air temperature barhta hai aur is se hawa ki humidity kam ho jati hai.
  • Nateeja: mesophyll cells ki surfaces se zyada pani evaporate hota hai aur rate of transpiration barh jati hai.
  • For every 10°C increase in temperature, the rate of transpiration roughly doubles.
  • Lekin jab temperature bohat zyada (around 40 to 45°C) ho jaye to stomata band ho jate hain. Ye closure plant ko apna much needed pani bachane deta hai, kyunke excessive loss detrimental hai.
  • Agar ye garmi lambe arse tak rahe aur soil mein pani kaafi na ho to plants wilt kar ke mar bhi sakte hain.
2. Effect of wind:
  • Wind is moving air, aur ye water molecules ke diffusion ko tez kar deti hai.
  • Is se mesophyll cells ki surfaces se evaporation ki rate barh jati hai, yani transpiration zyada.
  • Is ke bar aks jab hawa calm and still ho to water molecules ki harkat (diffusion) slow ho jati hai aur rate of transpiration kam ho jati hai.
3. Effect of humidity:
  • Humidity hawa mein water vapours ka percentage hai.
  • Dry air (low humidity) mein water molecules mesophyll cells, air spaces aur stomata se patte ke bahar more rapidly diffuse karte hain. Is se zyada pani zaaya hota hai aur rate of transpiration barh jati hai.
  • Humid air mein diffusion rate slow ho jati hai, jis se transpiration ki rate mein significant decrease aata hai.
Khulasa: temperature barhe to rate barhti hai (40 se 45°C par stomata band hone tak); hawa chale to rate barhti hai, still air mein girti hai; humidity barhe to rate girti hai, khushk hawa mein barhti hai.
Kitab ye bhi kehti hai ke inhi ke sath sun light, CO2 concentration aur availability of soil water bhi transpiration ki rate par asar dalte hain.
Q6. Explain the mechanism of food translocation by the theory of pressure flow mechanism. Translocation kya hai: prepared food (organic solutes) ka phloem tissue ke zariye plant ke mukhtalif hisson tak pohanchna translocation kehlata hai. Ye source (jo khana deta hai, jaise leaves) se sink (jo khana kharch karta hai, jaise roots ya storage organs) ki taraf hoti hai. Jo fluid behta hai use phloem sap kehte hain, jis mein about 10 to 25% dry matter hai aur us ka zyada tar hissa sucrose hai.

Theory ka naam: "The most accepted idea for how organic solutes moves in plants is the pressure flow or mass flow theory."

Mechanism ke 6 steps (kitab ke mutabiq):
  1. Loading at the source: source regions, jaise leaves ya storage areas, mein bani sugars ko companion cells phloem ke sieve tube elements mein load karte hain.
  2. Concentration barhna: ye active transport phloem mein sugar concentration barha deta hai aur water concentration kam kar deta hai. Is se phloem cells zyada "sweet" ho jate hain.
  3. Osmosis se pani andar: qareebi xylem cells se pani osmosis ke zariye phloem mein aata hai, jis se phloem cells mein hydrostatic pressure (pani ka pressure) barh jata hai. Yehi pressure sugary solution ko source (aksar patta) se door dhakelta hai.
  4. Pressure ka farq: high-pressure source se lower-pressure sink tak ka ye pressure difference hi translocation paida karta hai. Kitab ki misal: "It's like water being pushed through a hose."
  5. Unloading at the sink: jab solution sink tak pohanchta hai, jaise roots, to root cells organic solutes ko actively absorb kar lete hain. Is se phloem sap less sweet ho jata hai.
  6. Pani ki wapsi: phloem sap mein solutes kam hone se solute potential aur water potential barh jate hain, jis ki wajah se pani wapas xylem tubes mein chala jata hai.
Natija (kitab ke alfaaz): "So, it's like a system of moving food and water around the plant, driven by differences in pressure and the needs of different plant parts."

Figure 8.9 se madad: "Sugar is actively loaded into the sieve tube at the source. As a result, water moves into the sieve tubes by osmosis. At the sink, the sugar is actively unloaded, and water leaves the sieve tube by osmosis." Diagram mein source par leaf cells, sink par root cells, beech mein poore plant ki lambai mein chalta sieve tube aur us ke saath companion cells dikhaye gaye hain. Dono jagah (loading aur unloading) ATP darkar hoti hai.
