🔬 Hissa 1: Plant physiology kya hai

Chapter ka introduction (kitab p113)

❓ Sawal 1: Plant physiology kis cheez ki branch hai aur wo kis se deal karti hai?

Jawab. Plant physiology botany (plants ka ilm) ki ek branch hai. Ye plant ke jism ke functions se related sawalon ka jawab deti hai. Yani plant ke andar kaam kaise hota hai.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Plant physiology is the branch of botany, which deals with various questions related to the functions of plant body.

❓ Sawal 2: Wo kaun kaun se sawal hain jo plant physiology mein parhe jaate hain?

Jawab. Kitab ne khud chhe (6) sawal ginwaye hain:

  1. How plants grow from seed to the whole body. Plant ek chhote se beej (seed) se poora paoda kaise ban jata hai.
  2. How plants absorb water and minerals and transport them to the leaves. Plant paani aur minerals kaise absorb karta hai aur unhein patton (leaves) tak kaise pohanchata hai.
  3. How plants prepare organic solutes and transport them to the rest of the body. Plant organic solutes (khaana) kaise banata hai aur usay baaqi jism tak kaise bhejta hai.
  4. How plants exchange carbon dioxide and oxygen with their environment. Plant apne maahol ke saath carbon dioxide aur oxygen ka tabadla kaise karta hai.
  5. How plants adapt themselves in changing environmental conditions. Mausam aur haalaat badalne par plant khud ko kaise dhaal leta hai.
  6. How plants produce their young ones to continue their species. Plant apni nasl aage barhane ke liye apne bachay kaise paida karta hai.

❓ Sawal 3: Kya ye saare functions isi chapter mein parhne hain?

Jawab. Nahin. Kitab khud kehti hai: "Some of these functions will be studied in this chapter, while some have been discussed in other chapters." Yani kuch functions is chapter mein hain aur kuch doosre chapters mein pehle aa chuke hain.

🥣 Hissa 2: 8.1 Nutrition in Plants

8.1 Nutrition in Plants (kitab p113)

❓ Sawal 4: Nutrient kise kehte hain?

Jawab. Jo bhi cheez kisi organism ko uski growth aur metabolism ke liye zaroori elements de, usay nutrient kehte hain.

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho Any substance that provides necessary elements to the organism for growth and metabolism is called nutrient.

❓ Sawal 5: Kya nutrient organic hota hai ya inorganic? Aur plant nutrients kaun se hote hain?

Jawab. Aam tor par nutrient organic bhi ho sakta hai aur inorganic bhi. Lekin plant nutrients hamesha inorganic hote hain.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz In general, a nutrient may be organic or inorganic, however, plant nutrients are inorganic.

❓ Sawal 6: Nutrition kise kehte hain?

Jawab. Nutrition sirf khaana lene ka naam nahin. Nutrition mein wo saare processes aa jaate hain jin mein nutrients ko growth ya jism ki mukhtalif metabolic activities mein istemaal kiya jata hai.

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho The term nutrition is applied to all the processes that are involved in the utilization of nutrients in growth or various metabolic activities of the body.

❓ Sawal 7: Plants ko autotrophs kyun kehte hain?

Jawab. Kyunke plants maahol se inorganic nutrients lete hain, jaise paani (water), carbon dioxide aur kuch minerals, aur phir khud hi unhein organic compounds mein badal lete hain. Yani apna khaana khud banate hain. Isi liye plants autotrophs kehlate hain.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Plants are autotrophs as they obtain inorganic nutrients such as water, carbon dioxide and certain minerals form the environment and convert them into organic compounds. Kitab mein yahan "form the environment" chhapa hai. Wo asal mein "from the environment" hona chahiye tha. Choti si printing ki ghalti hai, matlab wohi rehta hai: maahol se.
🎯 Ek line mein Nutrient = zaroori elements dene wali cheez. Nutrition = un nutrients ko istemaal karne ke saare processes. Plant nutrients inorganic hote hain, aur plants autotrophs hain.

⚖️ Hissa 3: 8.1.1 Types of plant nutrients

8.1.1 Types of plant nutrients (kitab p113)

❓ Sawal 8: Plant growth ke liye kitne elements essential paaye gaye hain?

Jawab. Sixteen (16) elements. Kitab ki pehli line hi yahi hai: "Sixteen elements have been found essential for plant growth." In 16 ko do groups mein baanta gaya hai: 9 macronutrients aur 7 micronutrients.

❓ Sawal 9: Macronutrients kise kehte hain, aur wo kaun se hain?

Jawab. 16 mein se 9 elements plant ko kaafi bari miqdar mein chahiye hote hain, yani 0.05% dry weight se zyada. Inhi ko macronutrients kehte hain.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Nine of these are required in fairly large quantities (greater than 0.05% dry weights) and are therefore known as macronutrients. These include carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, sulphur, calcium, and magnesium.

Yani 9 macronutrients ye hain: carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, sulphur, calcium, magnesium.

❓ Sawal 10: Micronutrients kise kehte hain, aur wo kaun se hain?

Jawab. Baaqi bachay hue saat (7) elements plant ko sirf traces ya bohat choti miqdar mein chahiye hote hain, yani 0.05% dry weight se kam. Ye normal plant growth aur development ke liye zaroori hote hain. Inhein micronutrients kehte hain.

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho The remaining seven elements are needed in traces or small amounts (less than 0.05% dry weight) for normal plant growth and development that are known as micronutrients. These include iron, boron, manganese, copper, molybdenum, chlorine, and zinc.

Yani 7 micronutrients ye hain: iron, boron, manganese, copper, molybdenum, chlorine, zinc.

Poori tasveer ek table mein (ye table yaad kar lo, exam mein bohat kaam aayegi):

MacronutrientsMicronutrients
Kitne97

🧠 Yaad karne ki trick Macro = MORE, Micro = MINI. Macro wale 0.05% se ooper, micro wale 0.05% se neeche. Number bhi yaad rakho: 9 + 7 = 16. Agar exam mein 16 yaad aa gaya aur 9 yaad aa gaya, to 7 khud nikal aayega.
Micro ke 7 naam yaad karne ke liye pehle harf jor lo: I B M C M C Z (Iron, Boron, Manganese, Copper, Molybdenum, Chlorine, Zinc).
⚠️ Yahan ghalti hoti hai Micronutrient ka matlab ye nahin ke wo ghair zaroori hai. Kitab saaf kehti hai ke ye bhi "for normal plant growth and development" zaroori hain. Farq sirf miqdar ka hai, ahmiyat ka nahin. MCQ mein aksar yahi phansata hai.

❓ Sawal 11: Is chapter mein kitne nutrients ki ahmiyat parhni hai?

Jawab. Sirf do: nitrogen aur magnesium. Baaqi saare nutrients ka role grade 11 ki biology mein parhaya jayega.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Although, each mineral nutrient is very important for normal plant growth, here you will learn about the importance of only nitrogen and magnesium. The role of rest of the plant nutrients will be discussed in biology grade 11.
🎯 Ek line mein 16 essential elements: 9 macronutrients (0.05% se zyada) aur 7 micronutrients (0.05% se kam). Is class mein sirf nitrogen aur magnesium ki ahmiyat parhni hai.

🌿 Hissa 4: Nitrogen aur Magnesium

Importance of nitrogen in protein synthesis (kitab p113, p114)

❓ Sawal 12: Nitrogen macronutrient hai ya micronutrient? Aur plant usay kahan se aur kis shakal mein leta hai?

Jawab. Nitrogen ek macronutrient hai. Plant isay zyadatar mitti (soil) se, apni roots ke zariye absorb karta hai. Shakal hoti hai inorganic nitrogenous compounds ki, jaise nitrates (NO₃⁻).

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Nitrogen is a macronutrient in the plant which is mainly absorbed by the plant from soil through their roots in the form of inorganic nitrogenous compounds like nitrates (NO₃⁻).

❓ Sawal 13: Mitti mein nitrates ka sab se bara source kya hai?

Jawab. Dead organic matter ka decomposition, yani murda jaandaron aur pooron ka gal sarr jana. Kitab: "The major source of nitrates in the soil is the decomposition of dead organic matter."

❓ Sawal 14: Agar mitti mein nitrogen khatam ho jaye to kya karte hain?

Jawab. Aisi mitti ko nitrogen depleted soil kehte hain. Us mein nitrogen nitrogen fertilizers ki shakal mein daal diya jata hai.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz However, in the nitrogen depleted soils, it can be added in the form of nitrogen fertilizers.

❓ Sawal 15: Plant ke jism mein ye nitrogenous compounds kis kaam aate hain?

Jawab. Teen cheezein banane mein: amino acids, nucleotides aur chlorophyll. Aur phir un amino acids se protein synthesis hoti hai.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz In plant body these nitrogenous compounds are used to make amino acids, nucleotides, and chlorophyll. The amino acids are then utilised in protein synthesis.
🧠 Teen naam yaad rakho Nitrogen se banti hain: Amino acids, Nucleotides, Chlorophyll. Yaad rakhne ke liye ANC. SLO 3 ka sawal isi par aata hai.

❓ Sawal 16: Nitrogen ki kami itni khatarnak kyun hai?

Jawab. Kyunke protein plant ke jism ka sab se zyada paya jane wala organic constituent hai. Protein amino acids se banti hai aur amino acids nitrogen se. Is liye nitrogen kam hoga to protein kam banegi, aur plant ki growth bohat buri tarah ruk jayegi (severe growth retardation).

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz As the protein is the most abundant organic constituent of the plant body therefore, nitrogen deficiency in plant can lead to severe growth retardation.

❓ Sawal 17: Nitrogen deficiency ki sab se zyada nazar aane wali nishani (symptom) kya hai? Chlorosis kya hai?

Jawab. Sab se obvious symptom chlorosis hai. Chlorosis ka matlab hai plant ke patton ka peela par jana, jo minerals ki kami ki wajah se hota hai. Nitrogen ki kami chlorophyll banne ko rok deti hai (inhibits chlorophyll formation), is liye patte peele ho jaate hain.

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho The most obvious symptom of nitrogen deficiency in plant is the chlorosis i.e., yellowing of plant leaves due to deficiency of minerals, as the deficiency of nitrogen inhibits chlorophyll formation.
🖼️ Figure 8.1: Molecular Structure of Chlorophyll (kitab p113) Ye chlorophyll ke molecule ki tasveer hai. Beech mein Mg (magnesium) ka atom hai, jis ke chaaron taraf N (nitrogen) ke atoms hain. Rings par Roman numbers likhe hain: I, II, III, IV, V. Ek taraf R ka group hai. Tasveer ke saath do labels hain:
Chlorophyll-a (R=CH₃)
Chlorophyll-b (R=CHO)
Yani chlorophyll-a aur chlorophyll-b mein farq sirf R wale group ka hai. Aur dhyan do: tasveer khud bata rahi hai ke chlorophyll mein nitrogen bhi hai aur magnesium bhi. Isi liye dono ki kami se chlorosis hota hai.

Importance of magnesium in chlorophyll formation (kitab p114)

❓ Sawal 18: Magnesium macronutrient hai ya micronutrient? Plant usay kis shakal mein absorb karta hai?

Jawab. Magnesium bhi macronutrient hai. Plant isay bhi mitti se roots ke zariye leta hai, aur shakal hoti hai inorganic Mg ions ki.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz Magnesium is a macronutrient in the plant which is mainly absorbed by the plant from soil through their roots in the form of inorganic Mg⁺ ions. Kitab mein yahan Mg⁺ chhapa hai (ek hi plus). Chemistry mein magnesium ka ion Mg²⁺ hota hai, is liye ye kitab ki printing ki ghalti lagti hai. Exam mein biology ka jawab likhte waqt bas "inorganic Mg ions" likh dena, safe rahega.

❓ Sawal 19: Mitti mein magnesium ka sab se bara source kya hai?

Jawab. Dead plant debris ka decomposition, yani murda pooron ka koora karkat gal sarr jana. Kitab: "The major source of magnesium in the soil is the decomposition of dead plant debris."

⚠️ Yahan dhyan se Nitrogen ka source dead organic matter hai. Magnesium ka source dead plant debris hai. Alfaaz alag hain, exam mein ulta mat likh dena.

❓ Sawal 20: Magnesium depleted soil mein magnesium kaise daala jata hai?

Jawab. Do tareeqe kitab ne likhe hain: dolomitic limestone, ya phir magnesium fertilizers jaise Magnesium sulphate (MgSO₄).

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz However, in the magnesium depleted soils, it can be added in the form of dolomitic limestone or magnesium fertilizers like Magnesium sulphate (MgSO₄).

❓ Sawal 21: Plant ke jism mein magnesium kis kaam aata hai, aur uski kami se kya hota hai?

Jawab. Magnesium se chlorophyll banti hai. Is liye magnesium ki kami se bhi chlorosis ho jata hai, yani patte peele par jaate hain.

📖 Kitab ke alfaaz In plant body, the magnesium is used to make chlorophyll therefore, magnesium deficiency in plant can also lead to chlorosis.

✏️ Section I, MCQ 1 (kitab p126): Chlorosis does not occur due to the deficiency of A) sulphur    (B) magnesium    C) phosphorus    (D) calcium

Jawab: (D) calcium

Kaise pata chala: Sawal ulta hai. Ye poochh raha hai ke kis ki kami se chlorosis nahin hota.

  • (B) magnesium: ye to seedha kaat do. Kitab (p114) khud kehti hai "magnesium deficiency in plant can also lead to chlorosis". Yani magnesium se chlorosis hota hi hai, is liye ye jawab nahin ho sakta.
  • (A) sulphur: sulphur protein banane wale amino acids ka hissa hai, aur uski kami se bhi patte peele hote hain. Ye bhi jawab nahin.
  • (D) calcium: calcium ka kaam chlorophyll banana nahin hai. Chlorophyll ke molecule mein calcium hota hi nahin (Figure 8.1 dekho: beech mein Mg hai, aur N hain, calcium kahin nahin). Is liye calcium ki kami se patte peele nahin hote. Yehi sahi jawab hai.

Imaandari se ek baat: phosphorus (option C) ki kami se bhi patte peele nahin hote, wo gehre sabz ya baingani ho jaate hain. Yani ye sawal thora dheela hai aur do options bante hain. Kitab khud sirf nitrogen aur magnesium ka zikr karti hai. Exam mein calcium likhna, aur agar teacher ne tumhara C kaat diya ho to aaram se dalil de dena.

✏️ Section III, Q1 (kitab p128): State that nitrogen is important in protein synthesis and magnesium for chlorophyll formation

Ye extensive answer wala sawal hai, is liye jawab do hisson mein likhna. Poora jawab ye raha:

Introduction. Sixteen elements have been found essential for plant growth. Nine of them are macronutrients and seven are micronutrients. Nitrogen and magnesium are both macronutrients, and both are very important for the plant.

(a) Importance of nitrogen in protein synthesis.

  • Nitrogen is a macronutrient which is mainly absorbed by the plant from the soil through its roots.
  • It is absorbed in the form of inorganic nitrogenous compounds like nitrates (NO₃⁻).
  • The major source of nitrates in the soil is the decomposition of dead organic matter.
  • In nitrogen depleted soils, nitrogen can be added in the form of nitrogen fertilizers.
  • In the plant body, these nitrogenous compounds are used to make amino acids, nucleotides and chlorophyll.
  • The amino acids are then utilised in protein synthesis.
  • Protein is the most abundant organic constituent of the plant body, therefore nitrogen deficiency can lead to severe growth retardation.
  • The most obvious symptom of nitrogen deficiency is chlorosis, that is, yellowing of plant leaves, because the deficiency of nitrogen inhibits chlorophyll formation.

(b) Importance of magnesium in chlorophyll formation.

  • Magnesium is also a macronutrient which is mainly absorbed by the plant from the soil through its roots.
  • It is absorbed in the form of inorganic Mg ions.
  • The major source of magnesium in the soil is the decomposition of dead plant debris.
  • In magnesium depleted soils, it can be added in the form of dolomitic limestone or magnesium fertilizers like Magnesium sulphate (MgSO₄).
  • In the plant body, magnesium is used to make chlorophyll. Magnesium is present at the centre of the chlorophyll molecule (Figure 8.1).
  • Therefore, magnesium deficiency in the plant can also lead to chlorosis.

Conclusion. So nitrogen is important because the amino acids made from it are used in protein synthesis, and magnesium is important because chlorophyll is made from it. A deficiency of either of the two leads to chlorosis in the plant.
