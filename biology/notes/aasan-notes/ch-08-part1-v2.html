🍽️ 1. Nutrition in Plants (p113)

📖 Kitab ki definition Nutrient: Any substance that provides necessary elements to the organism for growth and metabolism is called nutrient.
Nutrition: the utilization of nutrients in growth or various metabolic activities of the body.

Plants autotrophs hain: wo inorganic nutrients jaise paani, carbon dioxide aur kuch minerals environment se lete hain aur unhein khud organic compounds mein badal lete hain. Yani apna khana khud banate hain.

📖 8.1.1 Types of plant nutrients Kitab kehti hai sixteen elements plant growth ke liye essential hain. In mein se nau (9) bari miqdar mein chahiyen.
  • Macronutrients (0.05% dry weight se zyada): carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, sulphur, calcium, magnesium
  • Micronutrients (0.05% dry weight se kam): iron, boron, manganese, copper, molybdenum, chlorine, zinc
🧠 Farq yaad rakhne ka tareeqa Macro = bari miqdar, Micro = traces. Line 0.05% dry weight par hai. Dono list mein 9 aur 7 elements hain, total 16. Aur yaad rakhein: har nutrient essential hai, chahe kitni hi kam miqdar mein ho. Kam zaroorat ka matlab kam ahmiyat nahin.
✏️ Do nutrients jo imtihan mein aksar aate hain Nitrogen (protein synthesis): plant ise nitrates (NO3⁻) ki shakl mein soil se leti hai. Nitrates dead organic matter ke decomposition se bante hain. Nitrogen ki kami ho to nitrogenous fertilizers daale jate hain. Plant body mein nitrogenous compounds se amino acids, nucleotides aur chlorophyll banta hai, aur amino acids se protein. Kami ki sab se numayan alamat: severe growth retardation.
Magnesium (chlorophyll formation): plant ise inorganic Mg² ions ki shakl mein leti hai. Source: soil mein dolomitic limestone, ya fertilizer Magnesium sulphate (MgSO₄). Magnesium chlorophyll banane mein istemal hota hai, is liye kami se leaves peeli ho jati hain (chlorosis).

🚰 2. Transport in Plants (p114, p115)

📖 8.2.1 Need of Transport, teen levels 1st level: material andar se bahar aur bahar se andar, taake har cell ko nutrients milein.
2nd level: jahan material banta hai wahan se jahan istemal hota hai, jaise jarr se patton tak.
3rd level: plant body ke andar se bahar, kyunke kuch compounds bekar (waste) hote hain.

Root aur root hairs (8.2.2). Root plant ka underground hissa hai aur mineral nutrients aur paani lene ka organ hai. Is ka surface area branching system aur root hairs se bara ho jata hai, jo absorption ke liye behtareen shakl hai. Dicot plants mein tap root hota hai (ek mota primary root), aur monocot plants mein fibrous adventitious roots.

🧠 Root ke andar ki tarteeb, bahar se andar Epidermis → Cortex → Endodermis → Pericycle → Xylem. Yehi tarteeb paani ka raasta hai. Endodermis ki deewaron par suberin ki pattiyan hoti hain jinhein Casparian Strips kehte hain.

📖 Facilitated diffusion Proteins within the cell membrane act as carrier molecules to help move nutrients across the membrane. Ye carrier proteins epidermal aur doosray root cells ki cell membranes mein hote hain.
📖 Osmosis, kitab ki apni definition The movement of water molecules through a partially permeable membrane from an area where they are more concentrated to an area where they are less concentrated.
Paani cell ke andar jaye to endosmosis, cell se bahar jaye to exosmosis.
⚠️ Ye farq imtihan mein poochha jata hai Root ki epidermal cells mein cell wall paani aur minerals ko azadi se guzarne deti hai, lekin cell membrane selectively permeable hai, yani wo sirf kuch cheezon ko guzarne deti hai. Cell wall aur cell membrane ko mat milayein.

💨 4. Transpiration (p117)

📖 Kitab ki definition Jarr se liya hua paani plant ke aerial parts tak jata hai, aur us mein se taqreeban 99% vapours ki shakl mein hawa mein chala jata hai. This loss is called transpiration.

🧠 Transpiration ko kitab "necessary evil" kyun kehti hai Faide: plant ko cooling deti hai; paani aur ghule hue minerals absorb karne mein madad deti hai; aur unhein upar ki taraf patton tak le jati hai. Is ke baghair plant zinda nahin reh sakti.
Nuqsan: itna paani zaya hota hai ke agar transpiration bay-qaboo ho jaye to plant wilting (loss of turgor) ka shikar ho kar mar bhi sakti hai.
Dono baatein ek saath likhna, tabhi jawab poora hota hai.

Leaf surface area aur stomata ka asar. Transpiration zyada tar stomata se hoti hai, jo patte ki satah par hote hain. Is liye jin plants ke paton ki satah bari ho aur stomata zyada hon, un mein transpiration ki rate zyada hoti hai; aur jin ke patte tang aur chhote hon un mein kam. Isi tarah din mein raat se zyada transpiration hoti hai, kyunke stomata din mein khule rehte hain aur raat ko taqreeban band ho jate hain.

💡 Ab shuru wala sawal

Darakht 400 litre mein se apne kaam mein laata hai sirf ek litre ke qareeb, kyunke 99% paani transpiration mein chala jata hai. Ajeeb lagta hai na? Lekin wohi 99% wo pump hai jo minerals ko jarr se patton tak kheench kar le jata hai. Isi liye kitab ise necessary evil kehti hai: nuqsan lagta hai, magar us ke baghair plant zinda nahin reh sakti.
