# Biology Chapter 7 — Metabolism

**Your complete study pack — designed to actually make this fun.**

Welcome! Metabolism sounds like something a doctor says before frowning at a blood report. But once you get past the jargon, it's one of the most mind-bending ideas in all of biology: **your body is a living chemistry set, running thousands of reactions every second, and every single one of those reactions is controlled by tiny protein machines called enzymes.**

Every time you move your finger, digest breakfast, or think about answering a question in class — that's metabolism happening. Right now. In your cells.

## The 7 topics in this chapter

1. **Metabolism and Its Types** — catabolism vs anabolism: tearing down vs building up
2. **Enzymes and Their Characteristics** — what they are, how they work, and why they're special
3. **Mechanism of Enzyme Action** — the lock-and-key story, active sites, ES complexes
4. **Factors Affecting Enzyme Activity** — temperature, pH, substrate concentration
5. **Enzyme Inhibition** — competitive vs non-competitive inhibition
6. **ATP — The Energy Currency of the Cell** — structure, the ADP cycle, high-energy bonds
7. **Photosynthesis and Respiration** — how cells make and spend their energy

## One thing to remember before you start

The entire chapter rests on **one single idea:** life is chemistry, and chemistry needs energy to run.

Your body gets energy from food. Plants get energy from sunlight. But neither can use those raw energy sources directly — everything has to be converted into one universal "energy coin" called **ATP**. Enzymes are the machines that do the converting. Photosynthesis and respiration are the big pathways those machines run.

So when you're reading about enzyme active sites, or glycolysis, or the Calvin cycle — always ask yourself: *how does this connect back to making or spending ATP?*

That's the thread that ties this whole chapter together.

Good luck. You're going to love this one.
