SECTION A — Multiple Choice Questions (10 × 1 = 10 Marks)
Q1. According to Surah Maryam's مضامین, whose miraculous creation is described in detail?   Answer: (a) Hazrat Isa (AS) 1 mark
The مضامین of Surah Maryam state that after narrating Hazrat Maryam's (AS) account, the surah gives a detailed description of the miraculous creation of Hazrat Isa (AS), born without a father, to establish that this does not make him Allah or Allah's son.
SLO: derive the مرکزی مضامین of the surah  |  Book p.6 (PDF p.9)
Q2. Who recited Surah Maryam in the court of the Najashi?   Answer: (b) Hazrat Jafar bin Abi Talib (RA) 1 mark
During the Hijrat-e-Habsha, when the Quraish sent envoys demanding the Muslims be handed back, Hazrat Jafar bin Abi Talib (RA) stood before the Najashi and recited the opening of Surah Maryam, describing Hazrat Isa (AS) as a servant of Allah, which moved the Najashi to tears and he refused to hand the Muslims over.
SLO: introduce (تعارف) each prescribed surah  |  Book p.4-5 (PDF p.7-8)
Q3. Surah Al-Anbiya is so named because:   Answer: (c) it contains the tazkira of many prophets 1 mark
The surah narrates the accounts of many prophets — Ibrahim, Ishaq, Yaqoob, Nooh, Dawud, Sulaiman, Ayyub, Musa, Harun, Zakariya, Yahya, Isa and Maryam (AS) — hence the name "Al-Anbiya" (the Prophets).
SLO: know the surah's name and meaning  |  Book p.34 (PDF p.37)
Q4. A hadith describes Surah Al-Anbiya as:   Answer: (d) an old and precious treasure (قدیم و قیمتی اثاثہ) 1 mark
Per the hadith quoted in the surah's تعارف (Sahih Bukhari: 4731), the Prophet (SAW) described this surah as "my old wealth and precious treasure" (میرا قدیم مال اور قیمتی اثاثہ). The book does not name who he said this to — that detail is outside what p.34 states, so it is left out here.
Distractor note: option (a) "قرآن کا دل" (the heart of the Quran) is the description the book gives for Surah Yasin, not Al-Anbiya — a different surah's fact, not a valid answer here.
SLO: introduce (تعارف) each prescribed surah  |  Book p.34 (PDF p.37)
Q5. Surah Al-Hajj consists of how many ayat?   Answer: (b) 78 1 mark
Surah Al-Hajj is a Madani surah of 78 ayat, per its تعارف.
SLO: introduce (تعارف) each prescribed surah  |  Book p.48 (PDF p.51)
Q6. In which surah was jihad first permitted?   Answer: (a) Surah Al-Hajj 1 mark
According to the مضامین of Surah Al-Hajj, permission to fight was granted here for the first time, and it was declared good news for the Muslims who could now defend themselves against the oppression of the disbelievers.
SLO: derive the مرکزی مضامین of the surah  |  Book p.48 (PDF p.51)
Q7. What does "Naml" mean?   Answer: (d) چیونٹی (ant) 1 mark
"Naml" literally means ant. The surah takes its name from the incident of the valley of the ants mentioned in ayat 18.
SLO: know the surah's name and meaning  |  Book p.88 (PDF p.91)
Q8. Surah An-Naml was revealed right after which surah?   Answer: (c) Surah Ash-Shu'ara 1 mark
Per its تعارف, Surah An-Naml was revealed immediately after Surah Ash-Shu'ara.
SLO: introduce (تعارف) each prescribed surah  |  Book p.88 (PDF p.91)
Q9. What is the خلاصہ (summary) of Surah Luqman?   Answer: (a) توحید کے دلائل، کردار سازی اور آخرت کی یاد دہانی 1 mark
The book's own closing line describes Surah Luqman's خلاصہ as "دلائلِ توحید، کردار سازی، اور آخرت کی یاد دہانی" (arguments for the Oneness of Allah, character-building, and a reminder of the Hereafter).
SLO: derive the مرکزی مضامین of the surah  |  Book p.137 (PDF p.140)
Q10. Surah As-Sajdah's other name is:   Answer: (b) تَنْزِيْلُ السَّجْدَةِ 1 mark
Surah As-Sajdah is also known as Tanzil As-Sajdah and Alif Lam Meem As-Sajdah.

SECTION B (a) — Short Questions (5 × 2 = 10 Marks)
(i) Main — What dua did Hazrat Zakariya (AS) make? 2
فَهَبْ لِيْ مِنْ لَّدُنْكَ وَلِيًّا ﴿٥﴾ وَاجْعَلْهُ رَبِّ رَضِيًّا ﴿٦﴾
"So grant me from Yourself an heir. And make him, my Lord, pleasing (to You)." Arabic 1Translation 1 (Surah Maryam: 5-6, Book p.15) OR — By what titles are Ibrahim (AS) and Musa (AS) remembered? Hazrat Ibrahim (AS) is called Khalilullah (the Friend of Allah) 1, and Hazrat Musa (AS) is called Kalimullah (the one who spoke with Allah) 1. (Book p.35)
(ii) Main — How do hypocrites worship Allah, and what is the result? 2
Hypocrites worship Allah "on the edge" (کنارے کنارے سے) — if good comes to them they are content, but if a trial befalls them, they turn back to disbelief 1, and so they lose both this world and the Hereafter, which is a manifest loss 1. (Surah Al-Hajj: 11, Book p.49) OR — Hazrat Sulaiman (AS) was blessed by Allah with wealth, kingship and great splendour 1, inheriting prophethood and knowledge from his father Dawud (AS), and was granted extraordinary miracles such as command over the wind, jinn and understanding the speech of birds 1. (Book p.88)
(iii) Main — Why was Surah Luqman given this name? 2
The surah is named after Hazrat Luqman (AS) because it narrates the pieces of wise advice he gave to his son 2. (Book p.137) OR — Allah sends a lesser punishment before the greater punishment 1, so that the wrongdoers may turn back from their sins 1. (Surah As-Sajdah: 21, Book p.146)
(iv) Main — How should we treat parents, per Surah Maryam? 2
We should be good and dutiful to our parents, as this is the way of the prophets 1. Hazrat Yahya (AS) is described as "barran bi-walidayhi" (dutiful to his parents) and was never arrogant or disobedient, which illustrates this 1. (Surah Maryam: 14, Book p.6) OR — Allah did not create the heavens and the earth for play or without purpose 1; His creation is entirely purposeful and points to the truth of the Hereafter 1. (Surah Al-Anbiya: 16-17, Book p.35)
(v) Main — Whom does Allah help, per Surah Al-Hajj? 2
Allah helps those who help His religion (i.e. strive for the cause of Islam) 2. (Surah Al-Hajj: 46, Book p.49) OR — The closing part of Surah An-Naml discusses the proofs and circumstances of Qiyamah, and the fate awaiting good and evil people on that Day 2. (Book p.88)

  • He thanked Allah for this favour and prayed to be enabled to do righteous deeds pleasing to Him and to be counted among His righteous servants. 1.5

  • Man has no protector or intercessor besides Allah. 1.5

  • This shows Allah's complete power and control over all creation. 1
(Surah As-Sajdah: 4-6, Book p.146-147)
Q.4 Main — Events of Qiyamah, per Surah Al-Hajj:
  • The tremor of the Hour will be terrible: nursing mothers will forget their infants, pregnant women will miscarry, and people will appear intoxicated though they are not, from the severity of Allah's punishment. 2
  • Some people dispute about Allah without knowledge, following every rebellious devil who leads them to the punishment of the Blaze. 2
  • The disbelievers will face humiliating punishment in this world and the severe punishment of the Fire in the Hereafter. 2
  • Those who believe and do righteous deeds will be admitted into gardens beneath which rivers flow. 1
  • Allah will judge between people on the Day of Resurrection concerning what they used to dispute about. 1
(Surah Al-Hajj: 1-2, 19-24, 69, Book p.48-52) OR — Tawheed and its arguments, per Surah Al-Anbiya:
  • If there were other gods besides Allah in the heavens and the earth, both realms would surely have been ruined/corrupted — the proof of Tawheed from cosmic order. 2
  • Allah is not questioned about what He does, but His creation will be questioned about their deeds. 1.5
  • The heavens and earth were joined, and Allah separated them and made every living thing from water — proof of His creative power. 2
  • Allah set firm mountains, a protected sky, and created night, day, the sun and moon, each swimming in its own orbit. 1.5
  • No human was granted immortality, answering the disbelievers' objection to the Prophet's (SAW) mortality; every soul will taste death. 1
(Surah Al-Anbiya: 22-23, 30-35, Book p.34-38)
