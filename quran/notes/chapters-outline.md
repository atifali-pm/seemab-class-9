### حاصلاتِ تعلّم (book p3)

The book prints one SLO list for the whole year, not per surah. After completing the Class 9 material the student should be able to:

- act on the آداب of reciting the Quran
- know the full names of the prescribed surahs and their meanings
- introduce (تعارف) each of those surahs
- derive the مرکزی مضامین of those surahs
- do the بامحاورہ ترجمہ of the prescribed surahs and understand them
- do the بامحاورہ ترجمہ of the selected آیات
- know the meanings of the قرآنی الفاظ of the selected surahs
- memorise, with translation, the قرآنی دعائیں that appear in the syllabus surahs

This is the SLO half of the exam-setting rule. The content half comes from the surah pages themselves.

## The 18 surahs

Eighteen surahs, in mushaf order, all مکی except الحج. Each one runs to the same internal shape: a **تعارف** page (surah name, مکی/مدنی, ayat count), then **مضامین / مرکزی مضامین**, then the Arabic text with the Urdu بامحاورہ ترجمہ underneath in the blue band, then **دعائیں** where the surah contains one, then **علمی و عملی نکات**, and it closes with **سرگرمیاں برائے طلبہ**, **برائے اساتذہ کرام**, and the **مشق**.

### Things worth knowing per surah

- **مریم** opens the book because of its Hijrat-e-Habsha context: the تعارف page runs the Najashi episode, which is why the model paper's very first MCQ asks which surah was recited in Najashi's court.
- **الحج** is the only مدنی surah in the year, and book p59 (PDF p62) adds a grammar table for ayat 65-69 (اَلْقَاطع / يُمْسِكُ / يَبْعَثُ / يُمْسِكُونَ), the only page in the book that teaches صرف this way.
- **لقمٰن** carries the نصیحتیں Luqman gave his son, and the model paper asks for two of them.
- **الفرقان** ayat 63-77 is the عِبَادُ الرَّحْمٰن passage, the longest examinable translation range in the book, and Q5 of the model paper offers it as a note topic.
- **دعائیں** boxes carry the duas the SLO says she must memorise with translation. **This list is incomplete.** The original survey only looked at the top of each page, so boxes printed lower down were missed; Maryam p15 and Al-Anbiya p45 were found on 2026-09-14 while verifying the exam-01 marking.
