# Tarjuma-tul-Quran Class 9 — Paper Pattern

Taken from the book's own **ماڈل پیپر برائے جماعت نہم** (book p214-215, PDF p217-218) and the **امتحانی پرچہ مرتب کرنے کے بارے ہدایات** (book p216, PDF p219). Both were read on 2026-09-10.

This subject does not use a pairing scheme the way Islamiat does. Instead the book prints a fixed five-question shape plus a rule for where each question's material may come from, and that rule is tight enough to act as one.

**Total: 50 marks.** Time is left blank on the model paper for the school to fill in.

## حصہ اول (معروضی) — 10 marks

**سوال 1: درست جواب کی نشان دہی کریں — 10 × 1 = 10**

Ten MCQs, four options each. Source rule: only the **تعارف، مرکزی مضامین، خلاصہ، اہم نکات** of the prescribed surahs. Not the translation, not the Arabic.

The model paper's ten give a good sense of the register:

1. نجاشی کے دربار میں تلاوت کی گئی: (سورۃ مریم)
2. حضرت عمر رضی اللہ عنہ نے کس سورت کو سن کر اسلام قبول کیا
3. سورۃ الفرقان میں رحمان کے پسندیدہ بندوں کی پہلی صفت (عاجزی)
4. نماز روکتی ہے (بے حیائی سے)
5. چیونٹی کا ذکر کس سورت میں (سورۃ النمل)
6. رومیوں اور ایرانیوں کی جنگ کا ذکر (سورۃ الروم)
7. حضرت لقمان کی وجہ شہرت (حکمت و دانائی)
8. قرآن مجید کا دل کہا جاتا ہے (سورۃ یٰسٓ)
9. قارون کا عبرت ناک واقعہ (سورۃ القصص)
10. الاحقاف کا معنی (ریت کے ٹیلے)

## حصہ دوم (انشائی) — 40 marks

**سوال 2: کوئی سے پانچ سوالوں کے مختصر جواب دیجئے — 5 × 2 = 10**

The model paper offers **eight** parts (i-viii), attempt any five. Source rule, per the instructions page, is two-part:

- (i) تعارف، مرکزی مضامین اور اہم نکات of the prescribed surahs
- (ii) بامحاورہ ترجمہ of the **مرکبات** of the selected آیات مبارکہ

So this question mixes content recall with small phrase-level translation. The model paper's eight run: two Luqman نصیحتیں, whose نبی جنات were تابع, صفات of رحمان کے بندے from الفرقان, a فضیلت of یٰسٓ, two موضوعات of السجدة, then three مرکبات to translate (اِنِّیْ عَبْدُ اللهِ، بَلَّغَ اَرْبَعِیْنَ سَنَةً، اُشْدُدْ بِهٖۤ اَزْرِیْ).

**سوال 3: درج ذیل میں سے کوئی پانچ قرآنی الفاظ کے معنی لکھیے — 5 × 1 = 5**

Eight single words laid out in a table, attempt any five. Source rule: only from the **ذخیرہ قرآنی الفاظ** prescribed for Class 9. The model paper's eight: اَلصَّلٰوةُ، كُنْ، اَخِیْ، يَمْشُوْنَ، مَعَ، اَلَّتِیْ، عُمْيَانًا، بَلَغَ.

**سوال 4: درج ذیل میں سے کوئی تین اجزا کا بامحاورہ ترجمہ کیجیے — 3 × 5 = 15**

Five passages offered, attempt any three, five marks each. This is the heaviest question on the paper. Source rule: **only** the examinable ayat ranges listed on book p3. Nothing outside those six ranges may be set here.

**سوال 5: درج ذیل میں سے کسی ایک موضوع پر نوٹ لکھیں — 10**

Two topics offered, attempt one. Source rule: تعارف، مرکزی مضامین، خلاصہ، اہم نکات of the prescribed surahs. The model paper offers سورۃ الحج and سورۃ الفرقان, each phrased as "تعارف، خلاصہ، مرکزی مضمون".

## What the school actually sets, which is NOT the book's model paper

The OPF First Term August 2026 paper (`seemab-reports/school/2026-08-first-term-exam/quran-paper.pdf`, filed 2026-09-10) keeps the 50-mark total and the choice-based spirit, but restructures the middle of the paper. Build practice papers to **this** shape, not the book's, because this is what she will sit.

| Section | Question | Shape | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | MCQs, on a separate sheet | not seen | 10 (inferred) |
| B | Q2(a) مختصر جوابات | 5 questions, each with its own OR | 5 × 2 = 10 |
| B | Q2(b) الفاظ کے معنی | 5 words, no choice | 5 × 1 = 5 |
| B | Q2(c) آیات کا فہم | 3 passages, each with its own OR | 3 × 3 = 9 |
| C | Q3, Q4 تفصیلی جوابات | 2 questions, each with its own OR | 2 × 8 = 16 |

Sections B and C total **40**, printed on the paper. Time allowed 2:15 hours. Class is written as **M-II**.

Three differences that matter:

1. **There is no translation question, and that is correct.** The book's Q4 asks for بامحاورہ ترجمہ of three passages. The school sets no such question; Q2(c) prints the Urdu and English translation under the Arabic and asks comprehension questions instead. **Seemab confirmed on 2026-09-11 that her class does not write tarjuma**, so the omission reflects what is taught rather than a gap. Do not add a translation section to practice papers. (Quran exam-01 did, and had to be re-sent without it.)
2. **Choice is per-question, not pick-N-from-a-list.** The book offers 8 and asks for 5. The school gives five separate questions each with its own OR. She cannot skip a topic area, she can only pick one side of each pair.
3. **The paper is bilingual**, every question printed in English and Urdu. The book is Urdu only. Practice papers should be bilingual to match.

Section A has never been seen for this subject, nor for any of the other First Term subjects. The MCQ count of 10 is inferred from the 50-mark total minus the printed 40, not read off a paper.

## Setting rules to respect

Carrying over the standing exam rules for this repo, with the specifics this book adds:

- **Do not set بامحاورہ ترجمہ questions at all.** Her class does not write tarjuma (Seemab, 2026-09-11). The ranges in the next rule are recorded only because the book prints them; they are not a reason to set translation.
- **Translation questions are hard-capped** to the six ayat ranges on book p3 (مریم 30-36، طٰهٰ 25-37، الانبیاء 30-35، الحج 65-69، الفرقان 63-77، الاحقاف 13-15). A translation set from any other ayat is out of syllabus even though the ayat is printed in the book.
- **Word meanings** come only from the prescribed ذخیرہ, not from any word that happens to appear in the Arabic.
- **MCQs and notes** come from تعارف / مرکزی مضامین / خلاصہ / اہم نکات, which means the surah's opening pages and its علمی و عملی نکات box, not the translation band.
- Unlike the no-choice format used for Maths and the sciences, this paper **is built around choice** by design: 8-of-5, 8-of-5, 5-of-3, 2-of-1. That is the book's own model paper, so a Quran paper should keep the optional questions rather than follow the no-choice house format.
- Every MCQ still needs both explicit book content and a stated SLO behind it. The SLO list is on book p3 and is reproduced in `chapters-outline.md`.
