💧 1. Displacement method (7.2.2, kitab p172, p173)

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein bilkul yehi likhna "We can find the density of irregular shaped solid objects like stones which can sink in the water. We need graduated measuring cylinder to measure volume and balance to measure mass of object." Aur aakhir mein: "...any insoluble irregular shaped object."

Kitab ke 6 steps (yehi likhne hain, isi tarteeb mein):

  1. Find the mass of the irregular shaped stone (balance se).
  2. Add some water in measuring cylinder and measure its initial volume (Vi).
  3. Tie thread with the object and lower it in the measuring cylinder.
  4. Water will rise (displace); measure final volume (Vf). Ye reading paani + pathar dono ka volume hai.
  5. Volume of object = Vf − Vi
  6. Density nikalne ke liye mass ko volume se divide karo.
Density of Solid = Mass of Solid / Volume of Solid  (Equation 7.4)

Figure 7.3 banana aa jaye: do cylinders. Pehle mein sirf paani, level Vi. Doosre mein pathar dhaage se latka hua andar, ooncha level Vf.

✏️ Example 7.4 (kitab p173): pathar ki density Mass = 80.52 g, paani 18 mL se 46 mL par aaya.
V = Vf − Vi = 46 − 18 = 28 mL = 28 cm³
ρ = 80.52 g ÷ 28 cm³ = 2.88 g/cm³
⚠️ Kitab ki galti: yaad rakho Kitab ka apna data 28 cm³ deta hai, magar neeche 80.52 ÷ 25 cm³ likh kar jawab 3.22 g/cm³ chhapa hai. Ye ghalat hai. Tum hamesha 28 se divide karna, jawab 2.88 g/cm³.
✏️ Example 7.3 (kitab p172): eent (brick) ki density, cm se metre Mass = 3.30 kg; 21.6 cm × 10.2 cm × 6.35 cm.
Pehle cm ko metre: 0.216 m, 0.102 m, 0.0635 m.
V = 0.216 × 0.102 × 0.0635 = 0.00140 m³
ρ = 3.3 kg ÷ 0.00140 m³ = 2358.773 = 2360 kg/m³
🧠 Do pakki baatein 1 mL = 1 cm³, is liye cylinder ki reading seedha cm³ ban jati hai.
Units milao: mass kg mein ho to volume m³ mein (jawab kg/m³); mass g mein ho to volume cm³ mein (jawab g/cm³). kg ko cm³ se kabhi divide mat karna.

🧊 2. Matter ki teen halatein (7.3, kitab p174, p175)

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein bilkul yehi likhna "Matter exists in three fundamental states; solid, liquid and gas. Matter consists of particles (atoms and molecules). Gaps between these particles is the main reason that divides the matter in three states. Particles of matter apply an attractive force on each other. The difference in strength of this force is the reason for smaller or large gaps between particles."

Asli farq sirf do cheezein: force kitni strong aur gap kitna. Shape, density, motion sab usi se nikalte hain.

Ye Table 7.2 + 7.3.1 ke points ek hi jagah. FYI (p175): liquid ke particles free move karte hain magar force ki wajah se container ke neeche rehte hain; gas ke phail kar poora container bhar dete hain.

⚠️ Kitab ki Figure 7.4 ke label ulat gaye hain "Solids" wale cylinder mein particles door door phaile hain (ye gas hai), "Gases" wale mein ghichi packed (ye solid hai). Jahan particles sab se paas paas hain woh solid hai.
