🧱 Hissa 1: Density

7.1 Density (kitab p168)

Ek sawal socho: 1 kilo rui (cotton) zyada jagah gherti hai ya 1 kilo loha? Wazan dono ka barabar hai, lekin rui ka bara sa dher hota hai aur loha ek chhota sa tukra. Kyun? Kyunke loha mein atoms bohat qareeb qareeb bhare hote hain (closely packed). Rui mein beech mein bohat khaali jagah hoti hai.

Bas yehi density hai: kisi cheez ki ek fixed jagah (volume) mein kitna maal (matter) bhara hua hai.

Kitab ke alfaaz (p168): "Density of a material tells us how much matter a substance has in its unit volume. The substance which has more closely packed atoms, has more matter in a fix volume. Therefore, it is denser substance." Metals aur rocks jaise solids dense hain kyunke un mein atoms qareeb qareeb hain. Jin cheezon mein atoms door door hain un mein fixed volume mein maal kam hota hai, is liye woh kam dense hain; isi liye liquids aur gases solids se kam dense hain.

Figure 7.1 (States of matter): "Solids have more atoms per unit volume than liquids and gases, so solids are denser than liquids and gases." Tasveer mein solid ke balls ek doosre se chipke, liquid ke thore khule, gas ke door door.

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho Mass per unit volume of the substance is called its density.
Density = Mass / Volume    ρ = m / V
  • ρ ko "rho" parhte hain. Ye density ka symbol hai.
  • SI unit: kg/m³ (kilogram per cubic metre). Chhota unit: g/cm³. Liquids ke liye aksar g/mL.
  • Density ek scalar quantity hai (sirf number aur unit, koi direction nahin).
  • Solids > Liquids > Gases. Solids mein atoms sab se qareeb, gases mein sab se door. Is liye solids sab se dense aur gases sab se kam dense.
🔁 Unit badalne ki trick (kitab ka FYI box) 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³. g/cm³ se kg/m³ banana ho to 1000 se multiply karo.
Kitab ki misal: "the density of petrol, 0.9 g/cm³, is multiplied by 1000 to write in units of kg/m³ as 900 kg/m³." Aur 1 mL = 1 cm³, is liye g/mL aur g/cm³ ek hi baat hai.
Dhyan do: Table 7.1 mein petrol 800 kg/m³ likha hai; FYI box ne misal ke liye 0.9 liya hai. Table wala number yaad karo.

  • Kisi cheez ka chhota tukra lo ya bara, density same rehti hai, kyunke density "unit volume" ka maal hai.
  • Temperature badle to density bhi badal jati hai (garam cheez phail jati hai, volume barh jata hai).
  • Liquids gases se zyada dense kyun? Liquids mein particles qareeb hain; gases mein door door, is liye fixed volume mein maal kam.

7.2 Density kaise maloom karein (kitab p169 to 173)

⚠️ Zaroori Kitab ke page 170 aur 171 (7.2 ka shuru wala hissa, aur liquids aur gases ki density) ki scan abhi hamare paas nahin. Photo milte hi ye hissa isi notes mein add ho jayega.

7.2.1 Regular shape (seedhi, pakki shakal, jaise cube ya eent): volume ruler se naap kar nikal lete hain.

  • Cube ka volume = (side length)³
  • Brick (eent) ka volume = length × width × height
  • Phir ρ = m / V
✏️ Example 7.1 (kitab p169): cube ki density Cube ki side 5 cm, mass 500 g. Density?
Given:m = 500 g, side = 5 cm
Required:ρ = ?
Solution:V = (side)³ = (5 cm)³ = 125 cm³
ρ = m / V = 500 g / 125 cm³
ρ = 4 g/cm³
✏️ Example 7.3 (kitab p172): eent (brick) ki density Mass 3.30 kg; length 21.6 cm, width 10.2 cm, height 6.35 cm.
Step 1:cm ko metre mein: 0.216 m, 0.102 m, 0.0635 m
Step 2:V = 0.216 × 0.102 × 0.0635 = 0.00140 m³
Step 3:ρ = 3.3 kg / 0.00140 m³
ρ ≈ 2360 kg/m³
🧠 Units milao! Mass kg mein hai to volume m³ mein lo, jawab kg/m³. Mass g mein hai to volume cm³ mein, jawab g/cm³. Kabhi kg ko cm³ se divide mat karna.

7.2.2 Irregular shape (tedhi meydhi shakal, jaise pathar): displacement method.

🐦 Pyaase kawwe ki kahani yaad hai? Kawwe ne ghade mein kankar daale aur paani upar aa gaya. Har kankar ne apne volume jitna paani upar dhakel diya (displace kiya). Displacement method bilkul yehi hai! (Ye kahani sirf samajhne ke liye hai, exam mein kitab ke steps likhne hain.)

Kitab (p172): "We can find the density of irregular shaped solid objects like stones which can sink in the water. We need graduated measuring cylinder to measure volume and balance to measure mass of object."

Kitab ke 6 steps (Figure 7.3: Finding volume of irregular shaped stone):

  1. Pathar ka mass balance se naapo.
  2. Measuring cylinder mein kuch paani daalo, initial volume (Vi) note karo.
  3. Pathar ko dhaage (thread) se baandh kar aahista se cylinder mein utaaro.
  4. Paani upar charh jayega (displace hoga). Final volume (Vf) note karo. Vf = paani ka volume + pathar ka volume.
  5. Pathar ka volume = Vf − Vi
  6. Density = mass / volume

Yaad rakho: ye method un cheezon ke liye hai jo paani mein doob jayen (sink) aur ghulein nahin (insoluble).

✏️ Example 7.4 (kitab p173): pathar ki density Rock ka mass 80.52 g. Paani 18 mL se 46 mL par aa gaya.
Volume:V = Vf − Vi = 46 mL − 18 mL = 28 mL = 28 cm³
Density:ρ = 80.52 g / 28 cm³
ρ = 2.88 g/cm³

🧊 Hissa 2: Matter ki states

7.3 States of matter (kitab p174, 175)

Kitab (p174): "Matter exists in three fundamental states; solid, liquid and gas. Matter consists of particles (atoms and molecules). Gaps between these particles is the main reason that divides the matter in three states." Particles ek doosre par attractive force lagate hain; is force ki taaqat ka farq hi particles ke darmiyan chhote ya bare gaps ki wajah hai, aur isi se un ki densities aur particle motion ke patterns bhi badalte hain.

Har cheez chhote chhote particles (atoms aur molecules) se bani hai. Ye particles ek doosre ko kheenchte hain (attractive force). Ye force kitni mazboot hai, isi se faisla hota hai ke cheez solid hogi, liquid ya gas. Force mazboot = particles qareeb; force kamzor = particles door.

🏫 School ki misal (samajhne ke liye) Solid = assembly ki line mein khari larkiyan. Apni jagah se hil nahin saktin, bas wahin thora sa hilti julti (vibrate) hain.
Liquid = break mein corridor. Sab qareeb qareeb hain, lekin ek doosre ke paas se guzar jati hain (glide).
Gas = chhutti ke baad ground. Sab door door, tezi se har taraf bhaag rahi hain aur takra rahi hain.

Kitab ke points, har state ke:

  • Solids (7.3.1): strong attractive force; particles closely packed, fixed lattice pattern; na gas ki tarah azad ghoom sakte na liquid ki tarah phisal sakte; sirf apni mean position ke gird vibrate karte hain; fix shape; high density.
  • Liquids: force gases se zyada lekin solids se kam (intermediate); faasla solids se zyada lekin phir bhi qareeb; particles flow and glide over each other aur apni jagah badalte rehte hain ("Liquid particles keep changing their position"); koi fix shape nahin, bartan (container) ki shakal le leta hai; intermediate density.
  • Gases: negligibly weak force; particles ka faasla un ke apne size se bohat zyada; constant random motion, aapas mein aur container ki deewaron se takrate rehte hain; force sirf takrane (collision) ke waqt; na fix shape na fix volume, poore container mein phail jati hai (volume = container ka volume); sab se kam dense.
ℹ️ For your information (kitab p175) "Particles in liquids move freely but stay at the bottom of the container due to a relatively strong attractive force, while in gases, particles spread, fill the container and move freely in it."
Yani liquid bartan ke neeche tik jata hai, gas poore bartan mein phail jati hai.
⭐ Point to ponder (kitab p176) Liquids aur gases ki koi fixed shape nahin, dono beh (flow) sakte hain. Is liye dono ko mila kar fluids kehte hain.
Trick: FLUID = jo Flow kare = Liquid + Gas. (Solid fluid nahin hai!)

7.4 Plasma: chauthi (fourth) state (kitab p176, 177)

📖 Kitab ki baat: exam mein yehi likho Plasma consists of positive ions, free electrons (negatively charged particles) and neutral atoms in gaseous state.

Plasma banta kaise hai? Gas ko garam karte jao. Particles ki K.E. barhti jati hai, attractive force kam hoti jati hai, aur particles zor zor se takrate hain. Takkar se atoms ke electrons alag ho jate hain aur atom positive ion ban jata hai. Is ionic state ko plasma kehte hain. Free electrons aur moving ions ki wajah se plasma current bohat achhi tarah conduct karta hai.

Kitab: "There is lot of plasma in the universe." Plasma is gas that hot, bright and highly ionized; "these characteristics together makes it different from the gas".

  • Aksar bohat high temperature, ya high pressure, ya dono par banta hai. High electric aur magnetic field se bhi ban sakta hai.
  • Plasma = gas jo hot, bright aur highly ionized ho.
  • 99% visible universe plasma se bana hai.
⚡ Plasma kahan milta hai (Figure 7.6: examples of plasma from daily life, aur Figure 7.7: examples of plasma in nature) Figure 7.6: neon plasma tube light, welding arc plasma, plasma lamps. Figure 7.7: the Sun, nebula, aurora.
Sun (stars plasma ki wajah se chamakte hain), neon light, lightning (aasmani bijli), aurora (north aur south poles par), nebula, welding arc, plasma lamps.
Gali ke welding wale mistri ki neeli chamakti chingari dekhi hai? Wo welding arc plasma hai!
⭐ For your information (kitab p177) Aurora (polar lights): Sun ke charged particles upper atmosphere se takrate hain to aasman mein rangeen roshniyan nazar aati hain.
Nebula: stars ke darmiyan dust aur gas ka bohat bara badal. Latin lafz, matlab "mist, fog". Is mein hydrogen aur ionized gases (plasma) hoti hain; andar ke garam stars inhein garam karte hain to laal, neeli, hari roshni nikalti hai.
🧠 4 states yaad karne ki trick Solid, Liquid, Gas, Plasma = "Sab Log Garam Pakore" 😄
Aur yaad rakho: Plasma = ionized state of matter.

🔥 Hissa 3: Temperature aur particles

7.5 Particles ki motion aur temperature (kitab p177, 178)

🍵 Kitab ki misal Pateele mein paani ke molecules hamesha hil rahe hote hain, lekin nazar nahin aate. Choolha jalao to paani garam ho kar us mein harkat (currents) nazar aane lagti hai. Garmi ne molecules ko tez kar diya!

Kisi cheez ko garam karo to do mein se ek kaam hota hai:

  1. Particles ke darmiyan attractive force kamzor ho kar bonds toot sakte hain (jaise melting aur boiling mein), ya
  2. Particles tez ho jate hain, yani un ki K.E. barh jati hai.
📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho The temperature of a substance is the measure of its hotness or coldness, and the temperature of a substance is directly proportional to the average K.E. of its particles.

Matlab: particles jitne tez, cheez utni garam. Garmi do: particles tez, temperature upar. Garmi nikalo (barf par rakho ya fridge mein): particles sust, temperature neeche.

Absolute zero: garmi nikalte jao to particles sust hote hote ek aisi haalat mein aa jate hain jahan un ke paas least ya no K.E. hoti hai. Na aapas mein takrate hain na container se, is liye pressure = 0 Pa.

📖 Kitab ki definition Absolute zero is the lowest possible temperature of a substance at which its particles have least kinetic energy.
Absolute zero = 0 K = −273.15 °C

Is se zyada thanda kuch ho hi nahin sakta. Absolute zero par particles ko hilane ke liye koi heat energy nahin hoti.

Figure 7.8 ke do graphs: (a) Kelvin temperature barhe to particles ki K.E. bhi barhe: seedhi line (straight line). (b) Gas ka pressure aur °C: line ko peeche barhao to −273 °C par pressure zero ho jata hai. Yehi absolute zero hai.

⭐ Can you tell? (kitab p183): bohat pasandeeda sawal Melting point aur boiling point par garmi dene ke bawajood temperature kyun nahin barhta?
Kyunke us waqt garmi particles ke darmiyan faasla barhane (force kamzor karne) mein lagti hai taa ke state badle. Ye garmi K.E. barhane mein nahin lagti. K.E. nahin barhi to temperature bhi same rehta hai.
⚠️ Kitab ki printing ghalti Is box ki aakhri line mein "temperature ... does change" chhapa hai. Asal mein "does not change" hai. Temperature melting aur boiling point par nahin badalta.

7.6 Internal energy (kitab p178, 179)

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein yehi likho Internal energy is the total kinetic and potential energy of the particles of a substance.
  • Potential energy particles ke darmiyan attractive force ki wajah se hoti hai.
  • K.E. teen qism ki: translational (seedha chalna), rotational (ghoomna), vibrational (thartharana).
  • Ideal gas mein sirf translational K.E. hoti hai, is liye us ki internal energy sirf K.E. ki wajah se hai.
📖 SLO wali line: zaroor yaad karo By increasing the temperature of a substance, its internal energy also increases.
💰 Misal (samajhne ke liye) Poori class ki "total pocket money" = har larki ke paise jama karo. Isi tarah internal energy = har particle ki K.E. + P.E. ka total. Garam karne se har particle ki K.E. barhi, to total bhi barh gaya.
🧠 Teeno saath chalte hain Temperature ↑ → K.E. ↑ → Internal energy ↑
Ek exception yaad rakho: melting aur boiling ke waqt garmi dene se internal energy barhti hai lekin temperature same rehta hai (upar "Can you tell?" dekho).
Kitab (p179): "A change in internal energy gives important information about the substance. For example, an increase in internal energy indicates an increase in temperature of the substance, which can be the result of energy given to particles by adding heat or by some other method."
Kitab ka sawal: "Can you name any method that can increase the internal energy of a substance without adding heat to it?" Jawab: us par kaam (work) kar ke, jaise dono haath zor se ragro (rubbing/friction): haath garam ho jate hain, garmi diye baghair.

🌡️ Hissa 4: Thermometer kaise kaam karte hain

7.8 Physical properties se temperature naapna (kitab p179, 180)

Garmi se particles tez hote hain, aur cheezon ki kuch physical properties ek pakke, pehle se maloom andaz (predictable way) mein badalti hain: volume, pressure, colour, electrical resistance. Inhi tabdeeliyon ko dekh kar temperature naapte hain.

📖 Kitab ki definition The physical property on the basis of which a thermometer works is called its thermometric property.

7.8.1 Liquids ka phailna (expansion): zyada tar liquids garam ho kar phailte hain. Jo liquid uniformly (barabar) phailta hai woh thermometric material ban sakta hai: mercury ya alcohol. Thermometer garam cheez ko chhuye to mercury phailti hai, tube mein upar charhti hai, aur scale par reading aa jati hai. Mercury ka phailna temperature ke saath linearly badalta hai.

🤒 Roz ki misal Ammi jab bukhar check karti hain to thermometer mein chamakdaar line upar charhti hai. Wo mercury ka phailna hai!

7.8.2 Gas ka volume aur pressure: gas garam ho to us ka volume aur pressure badal sakte hain. Gas thermometer do qism ke:

  • Constant volume gas thermometer: volume same rakhte hain; pressure temperature ke saath directly proportional. Law: Gay-Lussac's law. Garam bulb mein gas ke particles zor se takrate hain, pressure barhta hai.
  • Constant pressure gas thermometer: pressure same rakhte hain; volume temperature ke saath proportional. Law: Charles's law. Gas phail kar piston ko dhakelti hai.
  • Constant volume wale mein "we touch the thermometer bulb with the body whose temperature is to be measured." Garam hone par gas ke particles ki K.E. barhti hai, woh aapas mein aur deewaron se zyada zor se takrate hain, is se pressure barhta hai, aur pressure ki ye tabdeeli temperature batati hai.
🧠 Trick "Charles ke paas PC hai": Pressure Constant = Charles's law.
Baqi bacha: Volume constant = Gay-Lussac's law.

7.8.3 Liquid crystals ka rang: liquid crystal ek plastic strip ya patch mein band hota hai. Jism se lagao, temperature badle to rang (colour) badalta hai. Rang ko temperature scale se milao aur temperature maloom. Use: fever thermometers, aquariums, baby bottles (kitab mein bache ke maathe par strip ki tasveer hai).

Figure 7.9 (Thermometers): (a) mercury thermometer; (b) gas thermometer: gas ka bulb "bath or environment to be measured" mein, scale, mercury reservoir, flexible hose, aur mercury ki unchai h; (c) liquid crystal strip thermometer. Kitab: "There are also other thermometers for example resistance thermometers, bimetallic thermometers, thermocouple etc."

7.8.4 Resistance thermometer / thermistor: temperature badalne se is ki resistance (current ke raaste mein rukawat) badalti hai.

7.8.5 Bimetallic strip thermometer: solid (do metals ki patti) ke phailne se kaam karta hai.

⚠️ Kitab ki ghalti (p180) 7.8.5 ki line mein bimetallic strip aur thermocouple ki baat ghalti se aapas mein mil gayi hai. Neeche wali Table 7.3 ka jora yaad karo, woh sahi hai.

7.9 Fixed points (calibration) (kitab p181, 182)

Socho tumhare paas ek ruler hai jis par koi nishan (marking) nahin. Kya us se kuch naap sakti ho? Nahin! Thermometer ka bhi yehi haal hai: scale ke baghair thermometer "valueless" hai. Theek scale banane ke liye do fixed points chahiye: neeche wala aur upar wala. Phir un ke beech ke faasle ko barabar hisson (equal divisions) mein baant dete hain.

📖 Kitab ki definition A fixed point is a standard degree of hotness or coldness.
  • Fixed points ko reference points bhi kehte hain: lower reference point aur upper reference point.
  • Fixed points accessible (asaani se mil saken) aur reproducible (baar baar wahi aayen) hone chahiye.
  • Fixed points tay kiye baghair temperature accurately nahin naap sakte.
  • Lower reference point is a standard degree of coldness = barf ke pighalne ka point (ice point): 0 °C, 32 °F, 273 K. Upper reference point is standard degree of hotness = paani ka boiling point (steam point): 100 °C, 212 °F, 373 K.
  • Figure 7.11 (Fixed reference points): teen thermometers (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin) par steam point aur ice point ki lakeerein, aur beech mein 100, 180, 100 divisions.
ScaleIce point (lower)Steam point (upper)Divisions

Fahrenheit32 °F212 °F180
Kelvin273 K373 K100
🧠 Trick Divisions = upper − lower. (100 − 0 = 100; 212 − 32 = 180; 373 − 273 = 100)
Kelvin = Celsius + 273. Fahrenheit hi akela "ajeeb" hai: 32 se shuru, 212 par khatam, 180 hisse.

🔬 Hissa 5: Achha thermometer kaisa hota hai

7.10 Sensitivity, range aur linearity (kitab p182, 183)

7.10.1 Sensitivity

📖 Kitab ki definition It is ability of a thermometer to detect small changes in temperature. We can say that it is smallest variation in temperature which a thermometer can measure.
  • 0.1 °C sensitivity wala thermometer 0.1 °C ki chhoti si tabdeeli bhi pakar leta hai. 1 °C wala, 1 °C se chhoti tabdeeli nahin naap sakta.
  • Sensitivity least count jaisi hai (analogous to least count).
  • Mercury thermometer sensitive hota hai: room temperature ke paani mein garam paani ke chand qatre daalo, mercury foran upar.
💍 Misal (samajhne ke liye) Sunar (jeweller) ka taraazu aur sabzi wale ka taraazu. Sunar ka taraazu chhoti se chhoti tabdeeli bhi pakar leta hai, woh zyada "sensitive" hai.

7.10.2 Range (Figure 7.12: ek clinical thermometer ki tasveer)

📖 Kitab ki definition The range of a thermometer is the range of temperatures that it can measure. It is measured by the lowest and highest temperatures that the thermometer can measure.
  • Clinical mercury thermometer: 35 °C to 42 °C (Fahrenheit par 94 °F to 108 °F).
  • Digital thermometer ki range wide hai. Barf mein 0 °C aur ubalte paani mein 100 °C sahi dikhata hai. Isi tarah range check karte hain.

😄 Clinical thermometer sirf insani jism ke liye bana hai (35 se 42). Is se garam chai ka temperature mat naapna!

7.10.3 Linearity

📖 Kitab ki definition Linearity tells us how equally and uniformly a thermometer shows the temperature variations.
  • Scale par nishan barabar faasle par (equally spaced). 0 se 50 °C ka izafa aur 50 se 100 °C ka izafa, dono same accuracy se naape jayen.
  • Bimetallic thermometers linear hote hain aur apni poori range mein barabar accuracy se naapte hain.
  • Kitab ka tajurba: room temperature ke paani mein bimetallic thermometer rakho, phir paani ko aahista aahista garam karo. Thermometer aahista aahista respond karega aur us ki sui (pointer) chalegi.
🪜 Misal Seerhiyan: har seerhi ki unchai barabar ho to chadhna aasan hai. Thermometer ki "seerhiyan" (divisions) barabar hon, yehi linearity hai.

7.11 Liquid-in-glass aur thermocouple thermometers (kitab p183, 184)

Kitab: "Liquid-in-glass thermometers and thermocouple thermometers are two different types of thermometers that are used to measure temperature."

7.11.1 Liquid-in-glass: lambi, patli glass tube, aakhir mein bulb; bulb mein mercury ya alcohol. Garam: liquid phail kar tube mein upar. Thanda: sikur kar neeche. Scale calibrated hota hai, liquid ki unchai se reading. Use: ghar, school, laboratories, aur kuch industries.

Figure 7.14: parts of glass thermometer: mercury bulb, constriction (prevent back flow of mercury), capillary tube, column of mercury, scale lines, stem, expansion chamber. Constriction ek tang jagah hai jo mercury ko khud wapas bulb mein girne nahin deti, is liye reading ruki rehti hai.

7.11.2 Thermocouple: do mukhtalif metals ki taarein ek sire par juri hoti hain: ye hot junction. Doosre sire (cold junctions) voltmeter se jure hote hain. Hot junction garam ho to metals mein free electrons behte hain aur hot aur cold junction ke darmiyan voltage ban jata hai. Zyada garam = zyada voltage; thanda = kam voltage. Temperature ki tabdeeli voltage ki tabdeeli ke linearly proportional hai. Voltmeter ko calibrate kar dete hain taa ke voltage se temperature parha ja sake.

Use: furnaces, kilns, engines, industrial equipment; agriculture mein mitti (soil) aur paani ka temperature.

Figure 7.13 (Thermocouple thermometers): do taarein, Wire Type A aur Wire Type B, ek sire par jud kar Hot Junction banati hain (neeche shola/garmi); doosre sire Cold Junction par copper lead wires se MILLIVOLTS meter tak jaate hain, jis par 829 °C / 1500 °F dikhaya gaya hai. Saath mein ek digital thermocouple ki tasveer hai jis ke do probes (nokili taarein) hain.

Long Response Questions (QIII): answer ke main points

1. Define density. Describe methods to determine densities of regular and irregular-shaped solids, liquids and gases.
  • Definition: Mass per unit volume of the substance is called its density. ρ = m / V; SI unit kg/m³.
  • Regular solid: mass balance se naapo; volume dimensions se (cube = side³, brick = length × width × height); phir ρ = m / V.
  • Irregular solid (displacement method): mass naapo; measuring cylinder mein paani, initial volume Vi; pathar dhaage se utaaro, final volume Vf; volume = Vf − Vi; ρ = mass / volume. Sirf insoluble cheezon ke liye jo doob jayen.
  • Liquids aur gases: ye hissa kitab ke page 170, 171 par hai jis ki scan abhi nahin; photo milte hi add hoga.
2. How would you distinguish between solids, liquids and gases on the basis of attractive forces between particles and the motion of particles?
SolidLiquidGas
Attractive forceStrongWeaker than solids, stronger than gasesNegligibly weak

3. Describe two different physical properties that vary with temperature and explain how these properties can be used to measure temperature.
  • Expansion of a liquid: mercury or alcohol expands uniformly when heated and rises in a thin glass tube; its position on the calibrated scale gives the temperature (liquid-in-glass thermometer).
  • Pressure of a gas at constant volume: heating makes gas particles collide more vigorously, so pressure increases; this change in pressure is used to measure temperature (constant volume gas thermometer, Gay-Lussac's law).
  • Aur bhi likh sakti ho: colour of liquid crystals (liquid crystal strip), electrical resistance (thermistor), e.m.f (thermocouple).
4. Describe the construction and working of different types of gas thermometers.
  • Constant volume gas thermometer (Figure 7.9 b): gas ka bulb us cheez (bath) mein jis ka temperature naapna hai; bulb ek tube se mercury ke saath juri, mercury reservoir flexible hose se juri, aur scale se mercury ki unchai (h) naapi jati hai. Volume constant rakha jata hai; temperature barhne par gas particles ki K.E. barhti hai, woh zyada zor se takrate hain, pressure barhta hai. Pressure temperature ke directly proportional (Gay-Lussac's law).
  • Constant pressure gas thermometer: pressure constant; gas garam ho kar phailti hai aur piston ko dhakelti hai; volume ki tabdeeli se temperature (Charles's law).
5. Analyze how the structure of a liquid-in-glass thermometer can be modified to improve its performance.
  • Narrow capillary tube → more sensitive (small volume of liquid reacts quickly and rises quickly).
  • Mercury or alcohol (expand more than water) → more sensitive.
  • Larger bulb → more liquid to expand → larger range.
  • Mercury (low melting point, high boiling point) → wide range.
  • Borosilicate glass instead of soda lime glass → more linear.

Numerical Response Questions (QIV): poore hal

1. Wooden block: 300 g, 150 cm³. Metal ball: 500 g, 50 cm³. Density of each, and which is denser? Wooden block: ρ = 300 / 150 = 2 g/cm³. Metal ball: ρ = 500 / 50 = 10 g/cm³. Metal ball zyada dense hai.
2. 500 mL cooking oil, mass 450 g. Density in g/mL? ρ = 450 g / 500 mL = 0.9 g/mL (paani 1 g/mL se kam, is liye tel paani par tairta hai).
3. Plastic box 70 cm × 10 cm × 30 cm, mass 2500 g. Density of plastic? V = 70 × 10 × 30 = 21000 cm³. ρ = 2500 / 21000 = 0.119 ≈ 0.12 g/cm³
4. Aluminium density 2700 kg/m³. Mass of a solid aluminium ball of diameter 25 cm? Sphere (gend) ka volume: V = (4/3) π r³. Radius = diameter / 2 = 12.5 cm = 0.125 m.
V = (4/3) × 3.14 × (0.125)³ = (4/3) × 3.14 × 0.001953 = 0.00818 m³
m = ρ × V = 2700 × 0.00818 = 22.1 ≈ 22 kg
5. Iron cube, side 10 cm, mass half kilogram, has a cavity inside. Volume of cube? Volume of cavity? Cube ka volume = 10³ = 1000 cm³. Mass = 0.5 kg = 500 g.
Iron ki density (Table 7.1) = 7900 kg/m³ = 7.9 g/cm³. Asal iron ka volume = 500 / 7.9 = 63.3 cm³.
Cavity = 1000 − 63.3 = 936.7 cm³
Kitab ka jawab 935.9 cm³ hai; woh iron ko 7.8 g/cm³ le kar aata hai (500 / 7.8 = 64.1). Dono tareeqe sahi hain, bas jo density lo woh likh do.
6. Stone 200 g; water level rises from 40 mL to 73 mL. Volume and density? V = 73 − 40 = 33 mL. ρ = 200 / 33 = 6.06 ≈ 6.1 g/mL
