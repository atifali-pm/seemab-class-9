📏 1. Density (7.1, kitab p168 aur p169)

📖 Kitab ki definition: exam mein bilkul yehi likhna "Mass per unit volume of the substance is called its density."
Density = Mass / Volume,   symbol: ρ = m / V  (Equation 7.1)
SI unit: kilogram per cubic metre (kg/m3). Smaller unit: gram per cubic centimetre (g/cm3). Density of liquids is usually measured in gram per millilitre (g/mL). It is a scalar quantity.

Matlab: ek fix volume mein kitna matter bhara hua hai. Jis cheez ke atoms jitne closely packed honge utni woh denser hogi, isi liye metals aur rocks dense hain. Kitab (Figure 7.1): "Density of solids is greater than liquids and density of liquids is greater than gases."

Do book lines: "Density of small amount of a substance is the same as the density of its bulk" (choti si cheeni aur poori bori, density wahi), aur "density of a material changes with the temperature" (garam karne par volume phailta hai, mass wahi, to density kam). Can you tell: liquids gases se denser hain kyunke un ke particles paas paas hain; increasing order = hydrogen, milk, mercury, gold.

🧠 Unit conversion, ye hamesha kaam aayega 1 g/cm3 = 1000 kg/m3. g/cm3 ko kg/m3 banana ho to 1000 se multiply karo (0.9 × 1000 = 900), ulta karna ho to 1000 se divide. 1 mL = 1 cm3, is liye 1 g/mL aur 1 g/cm3 ek hi cheez hain.

✏️ Example 7.1: Density of regular shape (kitab p169) Given: Mass of the material = 500 g; side length of the cube = 5 cm
Required: Density of the material ρ = ?
Solution: Volume of a cubic shape object = (side length)3
Volume = (5 cm)3 = 125 cm3
ρ = Mass of cube / Volume of cube = 500 g / 125 cm3 = 4 g/cm3

💧 2. Density of liquids (7.2, kitab p170 aur p171)

7.2 sab methods ka aghaz hai: liquids, regular solids aur irregular solids. Liquid ke liye do cheezein chahiyen, graduated measuring cylinder (volume) aur balance (mass).

  1. Place the empty measuring cylinder on balance and measure its mass.
  2. Add liquid in the measuring cylinder and measure its volume.
  3. Subtract mass of empty cylinder from the mass of cylinder and liquid:
    Mass of liquid = Mass of measuring cylinder and liquid − Mass of empty cylinder
  4. Measure volume of liquid from the measuring cylinder.
  5. To calculate the density of liquid, divide mass of liquid by its volume.
Density of liquid = Mass of liquid / Volume of liquid  (Equation 7.2)

Poora khel step 3 ka hai: balance khali cylinder ka mass bhi gin leta hai, is liye ghatana zaroori hai. Figure 7.2 ke do balance: khali cylinder (145), cylinder plus liquid (205); kitab ne dono ko galti se "Step 1" likha hai, doosra Step 2 hai.

✏️ Example 7.2: Density of liquid (kitab p171) Given: Mass of empty cylinder = 145 g; mass of cylinder and added liquid = 205 g; volume of liquid = 60 mL
Required: Density of the liquid ρ = ?
Solution: Mass of liquid = 205 g − 145 g = 60 g
ρ = Mass of liquid / Volume of liquid = 60 g / 60 mL = 1 g/mL
Kitab ka natija: 1 g/mL paani (at 4 °C) ki density hai, to cylinder mein jo liquid tha woh water hai.

🧱 3. Density of regularly shaped solids (7.2.1, kitab p171 aur p172)

  1. Find mass of the solid regular shaped object using balance.
  2. Calculate the volume of object using formula according to shape of the object.
  3. Finally calculate its density by using the formula.
📖 Volume formulas: yaad karna lazmi hai Volume of cuboid = Length × Width × Height  ·  Volume of cube = (side length)3
Volume of sphere = (4/3) π r3 (r = radius, yaani diameter ka aadha)  ·  Density of Solid = Mass of Solid / Volume of Solid (Equation 7.3)
✏️ Example 7.3: Density of brick (kitab p172, cm ko metre banane wali misaal) Given: Mass = 3.30 kg; length 21.6 cm = 0.216 m, width 10.2 cm = 0.102 m, height 6.35 cm = 0.0635 m
Required: Density of the solid object ρ = ?
Solution: Volume = Length × Width × Height = 0.216 × 0.102 × 0.0635 = 0.00140 m3
ρ = 3.3 kg / 0.00140 m3 = 2358.773 = 2360 kg/m3
🎯 Pakki trick: units pehle milao Jawab kg/m3 chahiye to mass kg aur volume m3 (cm ko 100 se divide kar ke metre banao, 21.6 cm = 0.216 m); jawab g/cm3 chahiye to mass g aur volume cm3. kg ko cm3 se divide karna sab se aam galti hai.
Irregular cheez (pathar) ka volume displacement method se nikalta hai: woh 7.2.2 hai, Part 7B ke notes mein aa chuka hai.
