# Chemistry Chapter 6 — Stoichiometry

**Your complete study pack — designed to actually make this fun.**

Welcome! Stoichiometry sounds scary (it even *looks* scary — ten letters, three syllables, weird Greek origin). But once you get past the name, it's one of the most beautiful ideas in all of chemistry: **how to count things you can't see.**

## The 8 topics in this chapter

1. **Empirical vs Molecular Formula** — the two ways to describe a compound
2. **Molecular Mass & Formula Mass** — how heavy is one "unit" of a substance?
3. **The Mole & Avogadro's Number** — chemistry's counting unit (THE big idea)
4. **Gram Atomic/Molecular/Formula Mass (Molar Mass)** — connecting grams to moles
5. **Formulas of Binary Ionic Compounds** — how to write salts like Al₂O₃
6. **Chemical Equations & Balancing** — atoms can't disappear
7. **Ionic Equations** — cutting out the audience, focusing on the action
8. **Molecular vs Structural Formula** — counting atoms vs drawing them

## One thing to remember before you start

The entire chapter rests on **one single idea:** atoms are too small to count one by one, so chemists invented a counting unit called the **mole**.

Everything else — empirical formulas, molecular masses, balancing equations, ionic equations — is just a different angle on that same idea. If you understand the mole deeply, this whole chapter becomes easy.

So when you're reading, always ask yourself: *how does this connect back to the mole?*

That's your secret weapon.
