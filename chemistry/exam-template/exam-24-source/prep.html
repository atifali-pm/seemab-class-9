1. Hawa kis cheez se bani hai

Saaf, khushk hawa mein taqreeban 78% nitrogen aur 21% oxygen hai. Baqi thori si noble gases aur carbon dioxide.
Zameen ke gird gases aur paani ke bukharaat (water vapour) ke parde ko atmosphere kehte hain.
Pollutant: “Anything that is in the air, water or soil which has a harmful effect on some part of the environment.” Yani jo cheez hawa, paani ya mitti mein ho kar mahaul ko nuqsan de.

Particulate matter: hawa mein bohat barik zarraat (soot, dhuan, dust). Ye phephron (lungs) mein gehrai tak chale jate hain: saans ki bimari aur lung cancer.
Carbon monoxide khoon ke haemoglobin se jur kar carboxyhaemoglobin banati hai, jo oxygen nahin utha sakta. Is se behoshi, dam ghutna (suffocation) aur maut ho sakti hai. CO colourless aur odourless hai, is liye mehsoos nahin hoti.

3. Pollution ke sources aur equations

Natural sources: jangal ki aag aur aandhi (dhuan, dust); volcano (dust, zehreeli gases, raakh); deemak aur gaaye (methane); aasmani bijli (nitrogen oxides).
Human activities: coal, petroleum, natural gas jalana. Taqreeban aadhi air pollution gaariyon se aati hai.
2C(s) + O2(g) → 2CO(g)   (kam oxygen)
S(s) + O2(g) → SO2(g)
N2(g) + O2(g) → 2NO(g)
2NO(g) + O2(g) → 2NO2(g)
Dhyan do: kitab ne pehli equation “C + O2 → CO” likhi hai, jo balanced nahin (left par 2 O, right par 1). Sahi hai 2C + O2 → 2CO.

4. Ground-level ozone kaise banti hai

Ye secondary pollutant hai: kisi jagah se seedhi nahin nikalti, balki doosre pollutants (nitrogen oxides aur VOCs) ke dhoop mein react karne se banti hai.
Dhoop mein:   NO2 → NO + O
Phir:   O + O2 → O3
Yahan akela O (atomic oxygen) sahi hai, kyunki NO2 toot kar ek oxygen atom deta hai. Baqi har jagah oxygen gas O2 hi likhni hai.

5. Greenhouse effect aur global warming

Dhoop zameen par aati hai, zameen garam ho kar infrared radiation chhorti hai. CO2 aur methane jaisi greenhouse gases is infrared ko jazb kar ke wapas bhejti hain, kambal (blanket) ki tarah, is liye kam garmi space mein jaati hai. Ye greenhouse effect qudrati hai.
Jab insan in gases ko barha deta hai aur zameen zyada garam hoti hai, isay global warming kehte hain.
Agar global warming jari rahi: zameen ka temperature barhega; baarish badlegi, kahin sailaab kahin khushk saali; polar ice pighlegi aur samundar ka paani barhega; atmosphere garam ho jayega.

6. Acid rain (sab se zaroori hissa)

Normal baarish ka pH 5.6 hai. pH 5.6 se kam wali baarish acid rain hai.
2SO2(g) + O2(g) → 2SO3(g)
SO3(g) + H2O(l) → H2SO4(aq)  (sulphuric acid)
4NO2(g) + O2(g) + 2H2O(l) → 4HNO3(aq)  (nitric acid)
Loha (iron): Fe + H2SO4 → FeSO4 + H2
Marble: CaCO3 + 2HNO3 → Ca(NO3)2 + H2O + CO2  (2HNO3 zaroor)
Jaandaron par asar: machhliyan mar jati hain, darakht tabah ho jate hain, jheelein aur daryaa itne acidic ho jate hain ke un mein zindagi nahin reh sakti.

7. Hal (solutions)

Catalytic converter (gaari ke exhaust mein): platinum, palladium aur rhodium. CO ko CO2, aur NO ko N2 aur O2 mein badalta hai.
Acid rain ke liye: kam sulphur wala fuel; power plants mein flue gas desulphurization (calcium oxide se SO2 nikalna); catalytic converters.
Climate ke liye: renewable energy (hawa, dhoop, paani), darakht lagana, electric gaariyan.
Photosynthesis hawa se CO2 nikalta hai: 6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2
Apni hifazat: N95 mask, air quality index dekhna, ghar mein CO detector.
