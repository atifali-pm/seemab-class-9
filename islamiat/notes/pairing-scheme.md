# Islamiat 9 (PCTB) — Official Pairing Scheme

Printed in the textbook itself at book p163-164 (PDF p167-168), under
**امتحانی پرچہ اسلامیات لازمی برائے جماعت نہم کی تیاری کے لیے ہدایات → ممتحنین (Paper Setters) کے لیے ضروری ہدایات**.

**Annual paper: 100 marks, 3 hours, three حصے.**

| حصہ | Q | Type | Marks | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| اوّل | Q1 | کثیر الانتخابی سوالات (MCQs) | 20 | Whole book. **2 each from Bab 1, 4, 5, 6, 7; 4 from Bab 2; 6 from Bab 3** (= 20) |
| دوم | Q2 | مختصر سوالات | 12 | Set **9**, student answers **6** (6×2). **Bab 1 and Bab 2 only.** At least 3 per bab, and at least one must come from الاسماء الحسنیٰ |
| دوم | Q3 | مختصر سوالات | 12 | Set **9**, answer **6** (2×6). **Bab 3 and Bab 4 only.** At least 4 per bab |
| دوم | Q4 | مختصر سوالات | 16 | Set **12**, answer **8** (2×8). **Bab 5, 6 and 7 only.** At least 3 per bab |
| سوم | Q5 | احادیث کا ترجمہ | 10 | **4** ahadith given, translate **2** (5+5). Drawn **only** from the منتخب احادیث in Bab 1, never from ahadith quoted elsewhere in the book |
| سوم | Q6 | شخصیات (مختصر نوٹ) | 10 | **4** personalities given from Bab 6, write notes on **2** (5+5). The four are picked one each from: (1) اہلِ بیت و صحابہ کرام (2) صحابیات (3) علماء و مفکرین (4) صوفیہ کرام |
| سوم | Q7 | تفصیلی سوالات | 20 | **4** given, answer **2** (10+10). Taken from the book's own تفصیلی مشق questions. Order fixed: (i) from Bab 1 **or** Bab 4, (ii) from Bab 2, (iii) from Bab 3, (iv) from Bab 5 |

## Two rules that are easy to get wrong

**Short questions are bab-locked.** Q2 may only touch Bab 1 and 2, Q3 only Bab 3 and 4, Q4 only Bab 5, 6 and 7. A short question pulled from the wrong bab breaks the scheme even if the content is in syllabus.

**No detailed question from Bab 6 or Bab 7.** The scheme says this explicitly: `نوٹ: باب ششم اور باب ہفتم سے کوئی تفصیلی سوال امتحانی پرچے کا حصہ نہ بنایا جائے۔` Those two babs are reachable only through MCQs, Q4 short questions, and Q6 شخصیات.

## Where the school diverges

The OPF First Term paper (August 2026) does **not** follow this scheme. It ran 40 marks across Sections B, C and D in 2h15m, used every question with an OR alternative, and set its 10-mark Section C as "Abu Musa Ash'ari (Bab 6) OR physical and mental health (Bab 7)". Under the official scheme neither of those may be a تفصیلی question. Abu Musa would be legitimate as a Q6 شخصیات note, the Bab 7 option would not.

So there are two different targets. Use this pairing scheme when building a full 100-mark annual-style paper, and mirror the school's own shape when building practice for a school term exam.
