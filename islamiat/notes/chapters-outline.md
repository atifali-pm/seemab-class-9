**Board:** Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board (PCTB), Lahore. NOT FBISE.
**Curriculum:** Single National Curriculum (SNC) 2022, printing carries the 2025-26 session sticker.
**Source file:** `islamiat/chapters/islamiat-9-pctb-full-book.pdf` (170 PDF pages, scanned images, no text layer).
**Page mapping:** printed book page + 4 = PDF page. Book p1 is PDF p5.
**Read status:** every page read as an image on 2026-09-10. Urdu OCR is not available on this machine (tesseract has no `urd` pack, ocrmypdf is broken), so all future work reads the page images.

Every lesson prints its own **حاصلاتِ تعلّم (SLOs)** in a box before the content, and closes with a **مشق** (MCQs, مختصر سوالات, sometimes تفصیلی سوالات) plus **سرگرمیاں**. That means both halves of the exam-setting rule (explicit book content AND a stated SLO) are satisfiable directly from the page.

## باب اول: قرآن مجید و حدیثِ نبوی ﷺ (book p1-11)

| Lesson | Book p |
|---|---|
| (الف) قرآن مجید کی تدوین اور اس کی حفاظت | 1 |
| (ب) حفاظت و تدوینِ حدیث (دورِ اول) | 4 |
| (ج) احادیثِ نبویہ + منتخب احادیث مبارکہ + الاسماء الحسنیٰ | 7 |

Key content: derivation of لفظ قرآن from قِرَاءَۃ; Surah Al-Hijr 9 as the guarantee of preservation; compilation in three stages (عہدِ نبوی memorisation plus writing on stone slabs, leather, date-palm bark and camel shoulder bones; عہدِ ابو بکر after جنگِ یمامہ on Umar's advice with Zaid bin Thabit as کاتبِ وحی heading the work; عہدِ عثمان unifying the ummah on one قراءت). The compiled مصحف passed Abu Bakr to Umar to Hafsa. Hadith types: قولی، فعلی، تقریری، قدسی.

The منتخب احادیث section holds the 20 hadiths that Q5 of the annual paper draws from, and the الاسماء الحسنیٰ list (40 names) that Q2 must sample at least once.
